## Implemented scope

**Historical packet and display detail follows.** In particular, bullets 9–14 below describe older packet/display contracts where they conflict with the current behavior. Use the [handoff](sprint-handoff.md) for current policy; retain these details as implementation history, not current claims.

The speech transport, acoustic rules, bubble policy and older U geometry in the bullets below are retained as historical implementation detail. **For current visual behavior use Latest owner feedback and untested refinements below; for packet privacy use Latest owner-connected direction below**. Older statements about own speech in vanilla chat, two simultaneous columns, muffled anonymity/no ID/no positional cue, text-only/no-header bubbles and short chevron cues are superseded.

- Under the experimental mind/ghost prototype gate, ordinary text chat from an **exact committed CHARACTER body** is canceled before vanilla broadcast and routed as server-authored, unsigned local speech. Supported speakers are lifecycle-enrolled human characters and operator-configured, speech-enabled debug CHARACTER mobs (for example, `Villager (N)`). Committed ghosts, invalid bodies, missing identities, and unsupported speakers fail closed; players outside committed control keep vanilla chat.
- Ordinary SAY and SHOUT recipients are in the same dimension and within **15 blocks of the speaking body** (body-range prefilter); the sender receives their own line. `!!` changes the shout verb/format, **not** the range. Nearby recipients also require an eye-to-eye sound test within **15 blocks**: each of five bounded, closely offset parallel straight probes can pass when its effective eye distance satisfies `D + 8.5 * sqrt(T) <= 15`, where `D` is eye distance in metres and `T` is metres traveled inside solid collision. There is no separate through-wall distance or thickness cap. A clear center line is not vetoed by an unused offset probe crossing an unloaded chunk. Exposed floor/roof Y-face crossings block, including diagonal entry-side/exit-top floor or slab crossings; an **internal Y seam between contiguous stacked full-height solid wall blocks** may pass, but both solid intersections count toward `T`. Contiguous stacked floors still deny. A full 1 m wall permits `D <= 6.5`; a full 2 m wall permits `D <= ~2.98` near faces; a full 3 m wall would require `D <= ~0.28`, physically impossible when crossing its full thickness. A thin quarter-block barrier permits `D <= 10.75`. These are bounded straight probes, not pathfinding around a full L turn; the test does not load chunks. Text is bounded, nonblank, and subject to a committed-attempt cooldown. This is not a global channel.
- Mode prefixes on committed character speech: `,` whispers clearly through **3 blocks**, delivers server-muffled text at **more than 3 through 6 blocks**, and delivers nothing beyond 6, subject to the same sound test. Muffled delivery uses an unknown speaker and redacts the actual name, entity ID, and coordinates on the server before sending; it does not provide a positional cue. The project uses deterministic masking that leaves **every fifth ASCII alphanumeric character** visible (not an SS14-style randomized graded scramble). Leading `>` is an **optional literal radio-prefix escape** to force local speech when text would otherwise begin with `;` or `:key`; ordinary speech does **not** require `>;` or any prefix. `;` (Common) and valid single-letter `:key` forms are radio attempts; their body is delivered **once as an ordinary local whisper** (clear/muffled range) regardless of whether remote transmission eventually succeeds. Currently no remote transmission is attempted or available, and no separate sender failure notice is sent; the sender can see their normal local whisper. Malformed prefixes fail closed; they must not leak into local or remote delivery.
- The strict typed `moonstation14:common` radio-channel prototype (display label Common, keycode `h`) is registered and synced as metadata. **No playable radio transmit/receive is available:** equipment/hand authority must first provide a verified, body-owned active transmitter and a compatible receiver. Telecom may be added later; neither a prototype nor its keycode grants radio access.
- The overworld-owned persistent identity ledger assigns a human name and readable color at verified first lifecycle enrollment, before CHARACTER authority becomes active. Explicit first mob configuration assigns a persistent per-type number and color by entity UUID. Committed speech uses existing ledger entries rather than allocating an identity on demand; reconnects preserve saved names, colors, and NPC numbering.
- Client presentation includes a colored speaker name in local chat, speech bubbles for *other* speakers with block occlusion, and a bounded **100-line, session-only** read-only speech review toggled with the configurable **U** key (default). Own speech remains in vanilla chat and U, with **no redundant own `You say` HUD cue** or overhead bubble behind the camera. The review is a compact nonmodal HUD overlay (not a Screen), with a **210 × 95 GUI-pixel maximum** and **0.8× font scale**; speaker name, verb, and body start inline, with no timestamps or session title. It uses the **same right-edge geometry** during gameplay and over the vanilla **T ChatScreen** (`ScreenEvent.Render.Post`), with a top-right fallback on narrow GUIs to preserve U visibility and the chat input; it is hidden on other menus. Default-sized vanilla chat is given a separate column where space permits; unusually large custom chat histories can still overlap. **PgUp/PgDn** navigate without intentionally skipping intervening entries; full long utterances remain in chat. A case-insensitive whole-name-word mention highlights text; an offscreen/occluded speech cue indicates nearby speech. Character names are **not** shown overhead. For clear peer speech whose entity is untracked, a short-lived, server-authored **text-only** bubble may appear at the packet position; a tracked ID/UUID or position mismatch disqualifies fallback, and anonymous muffled whispers have no bubble or positional cue. Bubble billboard scale uses **positive X, negative Y** with camera rotation, matching Minecraft name-tag orientation; the owner now sees bubbles after this fix, but reports serious layout/contrast problems (below). The subsequent client-only `LocalSpeechTranscript` policy allows **up to four bubbles per speaker**, evicting the oldest on a fifth; expiry is **4s through 40 visible code points**, then **+40ms per code point**, capped at **10s from 190**. This is an intentional departure from pinned SS14, not a change to the **1s server speech cooldown** in `LocalSpeechPolicy`. Opt-in **receiving-client** JVM `-Dmoonstation14.debugSpeechBubbles=true` logs placement/submission reasons no more than once every 5 seconds without names or speech text. The newer timing/layout policy and ongoing UI refinement still need owner retest.

## Boundaries and caveats

**Current visual surface (supersedes the older U/vanilla presentation descriptions below):** `LocalChatFeed` holds up to **100 interleaved, arrival-ordered** server-authoritative speech lines and accepted non-overlay system/command `Component` notices for this client session. The **U** viewer is visible by default, toggles off/on for this session, uses the fixed-bottom-right, **0.8× font** compact overlay in gameplay and over **T**, and is not a typing screen. Its current 22 GUI-pixel bottom inset and stable placement across widths/heights await owner retest. A client-only `ChatComponent` mixin hides vanilla history in-world during gameplay and T, regardless of committed status or U visibility; it leaves vanilla history storage, input, slash-command transport, suggestions, signatures, acknowledgments and logging in place. `LocalSpeechClient` feeds speech to U without adding a duplicate vanilla line. A composable `@WrapOperation` mirrors notices **after the NeoForge hook** only on the accepted non-actionbar `ChatListener.handleSystemMessage` branch; player or disguised chat is **not** reclassified from a system-looking tag or unsigned content. Actionbar display remains unaffected. Uncommitted player chat may be visually hidden under this approved policy; it is not deleted or promoted to authoritative character speech. Invisible vanilla click styles/links, queued-message clicks and tag hovers are suppressed; vanilla PageUp/wheel scroll is redirected to the U feed while the history is hidden. The notice feed is **read-only**, so clickable vanilla notice components do not become clickable in U. Other mods inserting messages directly through local `ChatComponent.addMessage` may not be mirrored. Turning U off hides both visual history surfaces in-world. Render/mixin behavior and actual pixels still require connected-client verification.

- No playable radio, voice, alias item, or inspection workflow is included; **do not claim a radio demo**. Hearing a radio attempt's local whisper over the air is not evidence that the listener owns or can receive that radio channel. Future successful radio transmission must retain the one local whisper, not replace or duplicate it. There is no automatic migration of old profiles or already-configured mobs whose ledger entries are missing; those paths must not invent a new identity. An unreadable or incompatible ledger also fails closed rather than being silently replaced.
- Sound testing is approximate: neighbor-dependent block shapes and nearby straight offset probes do not model propagation fully around corners. Connected checks of the revised sound boundaries and two-client bubble layout/contrast/timing remain pending; earlier observed local radio whispers do not prove remote radio. A **unified U input** was a separate design idea, not part of the approved single visual surface: vanilla T typing and `/` commands remain available. Do not imply the U viewer replaces text entry.
- Controlled chat is delivered as **unsigned local/system content**, not vanilla signed player chat, so it does **not** participate in vanilla signed-chat reporting. Cancellation is registered at highest event priority, but another mod at the **same priority** may still observe the original event/message first; do not assume exclusive interception.
- The review is local to the current client session and clears on login, logout, or world change; it is not server-side archival. Presentation code and unit coverage do not establish that the in-game visual UI has been validated.
- The earlier U side-column separation from vanilla chat was **not** a universal non-overlap guarantee on narrow GUIs (approximately 440 pixels or less). In the current unified in-world presentation vanilla history is hidden; the older overlap limitation is historical. Connected-client visual readability remains unverified.
- Tracked peer bubbles now use `entity.getPosition(partialTick)` for rendered placement, matched to `getEyePosition(partialTick)` and the render event camera position. The tick position remains authoritative for UUID/position binding; an untracked speaker still uses the static server packet-position fallback. Headless tests exercise motion at partial ticks **0, 0.5, and 1**, but do not prove smooth on-screen movement or eliminate offscreen placement.
