# Parity decisions and acceptance ledger

Target: SS14 `c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91`. Implementation authorized 2026-10-03. See `PLAN.md` for scope.

**Subsequent authorized departure:** pressure-driven normal transport and pressure-compatible LINDA are now being implemented. See `../pressure-transport-sprint/DECISIONS.md`. The source mole-average and archived-capacity equations documented below remain reference kernels, but are not the new production normal-transport rules. Space/explosive flow and chemistry remain separate and unchanged by that departure. Pressure-mode acceptance is tracked separately; the earlier green gates below do not validate it.

## Decisions for user judgement

1. **Numerical API boundary:** prefer float simulation storage/calculation while allowing existing double-valued Java entry points where this avoids unrelated consumer changes. This is not a promise of cross-runtime bitwise identity.
2. **Minecraft geometry:** preserve six-face loaded-only topology and existing finite/exterior ownership proofs. Adapt floor/map identity to 3D world classification; do not replace enclosure detection merely to copy a 2D grid.
3. **Upstream dimensionally inconsistent spacing quantities:** when a local mole-derived quantity is used as upstream target pressure, first express it in equivalent 2,500 L reference inventory. This preserves same-concentration pressure behavior despite 1,000 L local cells. Do not silently repair the upstream mole-as-pressure equation.
4. **Scope:** development protection, bespoke test fixture design, wind/entity pressure movement, generalized ambient explosive discharge, pipes/full devices, and disabled-by-default superconductivity remain deferred. Focused numerical tests and existing integration fixtures are still required evidence for changed code.
5. **Fire sequencing:** foundation first; then source-faithful hotspot lifecycle/chemistry integration. Conservation diagnostics are observers, not permission to replace upstream artificial heating/cooling/cleanup behavior.
6. **Test devices:** keep these explicitly local test tools, not claimed SS14 device ports. Decouple their real-time rates from atmosphere cycle frequency by using 20 TPS external writes with unchanged per-second rates/caps. Full upstream device-stage integration is deferred with pipes/full devices.
7. **Startup profiles:** reuse existing dimension configuration for the currently needed breathable/vacuum gas profiles and add a separate Space dimension list. No generalized profile prototype family is introduced without an authoring requirement. The two lists are independent, including breathable Space and vacuum non-Space combinations.
8. **Hotspot bypass:** direct source verification corrected an initial research report: the bypass branch does not call React a second time. It consumes the prior whole-air LINDA reaction result; only the non-bypass portion gets another shared reaction pass.
9. **Effective thermal capacity:** upstream ordinary heat capacity has a normalized 0.0003 floor, while an empty immutable reservoir has a special normalized capacity of 7,000. Preserve the distinction from raw physical Cp. Translate that artificial reference-tile reservoir capacity by 0.4 to 2,800 for local cells, maintaining relative cooling behavior against volume-scaled gas inventory. This is an explicit Minecraft conversion decision, not a new claim that vacuum physically stores heat.
10. **Hotspot ignitability:** upstream masked fuel and oxidizer sums must each exceed its canonical 0.5 mol threshold; translate to 0.2 mol locally. Trace fuel/oxygen greater than zero alone does not qualify. The existence/ignition/spread temperature inequalities remain distinct.
11. **Reaction-result state:** preserve SS14's single persistent Fire result slot, including resets by invoked zero-extent fire effects and retention when no fire effect runs. Removed portions start fresh at zero; bypass reads the whole-cell component. Reaction events remain useful diagnostics but are not summed to determine growth.
12. **Groups and reservoirs:** immutable exterior neighbors participate in group membership and averaging counts, but never receive copied gas state. Remove the invented global 800-cell membership ceiling. Track group pass identity across yields so resuming one breakdown does not tick unrelated groups again.
13. **Cross-yield LINDA:** archive identity alone is insufficient; preserve processed-cell identity too. Skip already processed neighbors across batches, not just edges that previously qualified for sharing.
14. **Normal ambient Monstermos:** source verification corrected the earlier incomplete description: immutable non-Space endpoints also participate in normal Monstermos averaging/routing. Keep them immutable during publication. Model the Minecraft grid boundary as unique one-layer loaded exterior endpoints adjacent to finite cells, not an unbounded traversal of every outside air block. This is a geometry adapter, not generalized explosive discharge into ambient.
15. **Grenade volume adapter:** pinned `ExplosionSystem.Processing.cs:244` passes explosion intensity directly as HotspotExpose volume. Treat that source input as a reference-tile exposure quantity: convert intensity ×0.4 to local exposed liters, then apply ×25 only when creating new hotspot metadata. Do not multiply by cell liters or pre-apply ×25 in queued decisions. Boosting an existing hotspot compares against the unmultiplied exposed volume, as upstream does.
16. **Cadence correction:** pinned Processing.cs consumes one interval per stage invocation, not per completed cycle. `RealAtmosTime()` explicitly multiplies interval by enabled stage count. With DeltaPressureDamage enabled and superconductivity disabled, there are nine stages: 9/15 = 0.6 seconds per nominal full cycle. Preserve stage timing slots for deferred systems, retain elapsed debt, and resume within stage under the budget. Earlier claims of 15 completed cycles/s and a ninefold slower old group timer were wrong; the user was informed during implementation.
17. **Visual acceptance boundary:** this foundation pass preserves the existing server-authoritative billboard bridge, not full SS14 visual parity. Upstream `Effects/fire.rsi` has states 1/2 (>40% volume) and 3 (bypass), directional animations and per-frame delays; the current client uses a vanilla sprite and intensity payload. Replacing that payload/asset is a distinct visual follow-up, not grounds to alter numerical hotspot behavior. Prefer the pinned RSI with its CC-BY-SA-3.0/tgstation attribution when that follow-up is undertaken.
18. **Tile-fire entity exposure:** emit the gameplay exposure after a valid committed hotspot processing pass, including bypass/no-new-reaction and zero-result passes. First-process skips and fizzles do not emit it. Use post-hotspot metadata temperature, not whole-cell temperature or the existence of chemical reaction events. This follows `PerformHotspotExposure`'s `TileFireEvent`; the accepted one-second fire-stack damage cadence is unchanged.
19. **World removal cleanup:** use the same split contract for proportional sinks and finite/immutable breath samples. Packet and remainder cleanup are independent; a sub-cutoff inhaled trace may be discarded while its source remainder survives. The prior eye-cell trace-dose estimate did not account for this removal cleanup and is not an upstream numerical oracle. The reported live radiation discrepancy remains a separate unaccepted issue.
20. **Exposure cycle provenance:** new hotspot metadata inherits whether its recipient actually completed LINDA this cycle, matching `CurrentCycle > UpdateCounter`. Such a recipient has already skipped its first processing; an unprocessed recipient still needs the first-process skip. Neither flag permits a newly spread receiver into the current HOTSPOTS snapshot. Existing-hotspot boosts preserve their flag.
21. **Space-flow bookkeeping is not LINDA processing:** upstream Space progression explicitly reactivates finite tiles; Monstermos `LastCycle` is distinct from LINDA `CurrentCycle`. Both normal and Space equalization leave finite cells eligible for LINDA, including a Space no-transfer pass below ReleaseGasTo's 10 kPa friction guard. Suppressing that later sharing can strand low-pressure room gas.
22. **Newly valid hotspot barrier:** a position in a generic reactive-work snapshot is not necessarily an existing hotspot. Creating receiver metadata removes that position from the current HOTSPOTS pass even if it was previously admitted as generic reactive work. Preserve its activation for a later pass and do not displace other original sources.
23. **Archived-capacity conduction is not exact raw-energy conservation:** upstream moves live species but applies conduction temperature changes using archived capacities. A two-cell numerical reference pins this explicitly: archived 43 mol hot N₂ / 45 mol cold N₂, followed by a 0.1 mol hot-cell removal and Share, produces more than 1 MJ of raw-energy loss without clamping or a microscopic floor. Do not change source equations or conceal this with a broad floating-point tolerance. Continuous-writer world tests retain thermal progress, measured removals, inventory and no-export checks; their raw-energy residual is an observation, not a physical-conservation oracle.
24. **Realized immutable-reservoir accounting:** observers record the finite-side species/energy difference for boundary-coupled LINDA sharing and immutable-member group averaging. LINDA accounting uses the shared result before whole-air chemistry; group results emit their exchange only on completed averaging. Publish either only after successful gas transactions. Pure finite-group numerical differences are not invented boundary exchange. These are realized finite-side deltas, not a claim that every reported joule came from a physical exported packet.
25. **Stage admission is not a mandatory batch yield:** station-scale follow-up restores multiple bounded Monstermos slices/solves within the shared soft 3 ms admission budget, matching upstream `Processing.cs:299–321`. Ownership likewise reuses its REVALIDATE budget, with progress/failed-proof guards. The local 384-probe and 128-claim limits become per-slice limits; their values, proof safety, 15 Hz admission rate and one-stage-per-callback boundary remain unchanged.
26. **Processed membership is the solver's membership:** normal Monstermos marks only its first-200 included finite gas nodes, not the service's larger finite input. Immutable endpoints consume slots in that included set. Inactive source membership is source-only; successful publication precedes processed bookkeeping. Omitted finite nodes remain eligible.
27. **Processed seeds are not topology barriers:** a previous same-cycle solve does not make its cells blocked neighbors. Upstream increments BFS identity per solve and guards only same-solve revisits (`Monstermos.cs:61–90`), while `LastCycle` guards the next seed. Restore overlapping solves across patch seams while retaining the processed-seed and per-discovery visited guards.
28. **Local LINDA preparation stays local:** a one-source Share/React batch must not materialize the entire cycle archive or copy accumulated processed membership. Resolve only referenced archived cells and use read-only prior membership plus local processed additions. This preserves source ordering and once-cycle archival semantics while removing quadratic per-cycle preparation and allocation.
29. **Skip already-classified ownership seeds:** queued seeds now covered by valid loaded/passable exterior classification must not start redundant searches/proofs. Keep finite precedence, proof invalidation/retry behavior and the 4,096-reference cap. Do not replace that cap with a unique-cell-only budget that permits unbounded overlapping proof references.
