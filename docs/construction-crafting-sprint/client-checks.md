# Connected-client checks

Use a controlled character with body-owned materials/tools. Follow the configured bindings shown in the HUD; G/R/H below are defaults.

1. Open storage, then tap G. Icons/labels should sit lower without bleeding into neighboring rows. Girder instructions show only “2 Steel”; Steel Wall shows “1) 2 Steel” and “2) 2 Steel”, without repeated names, output, Add/Step labels or times. Metal Rod remains “1 Steel → 2 Metal Rods”, with quantity centered between −/+. Warnings stay above Craft; the list keeps its height and reaches its final entry.
2. With search unfocused, press G again to close the browser; hold/release must not reopen it. A fresh tap opens it again. Repeat with storage open and the cursor locked. Click the search bar from an unfocused state, then type Plastic while browsing pipes. Clear it; search pipes while browsing walls. Test Backspace and G/R/H/W typing without gameplay actions. Drag the browser; pause/resume; move it top-right and lock/unlock twice. Clicking storage must not hide uncovered browser regions. Outside-window clicks lock without acting; Select plan closes/locks.
3. Select a disposal plan, hold G and scroll through all nine variants in both directions. Try slow single notches and rapid scrolling, including wraparound; selection/highlight must not bounce after acknowledgements. No misleading Select/Close footer. R rotates; H exits. The narrow banner avoids chat, with red/partial-yellow/green material counts and no Ready/Insufficient text.
4. Check green translucent ghosts for both girders and pipes with enough material; check blocked/insufficient states and unrelated-tool suppression. Start two placements at different spots using 4 steel: both must finish, with visible markers at both spots. Pipe foundations take one second too. Cancel work by moving and verify reserved materials return.
5. With Girder selected, use steel on top of an existing girder: it must attempt a new girder, not finish a wall. Repeat above a player-built steel wall and a generated wall. Select a steel-wall goal and place its foundation; aim for 0.5 seconds. Guidance shows only the goal and next requirement, with space above its bottom border. Look away to dismiss; changing plans must not rewrite its goal.
6. Exit construction mode with H. Start wall progression on four different girders without moving, using steel/plastic. All four local timer frames should appear immediately and progress independently; workspot markers remain. Each wall finishes normally. Pry/weld back to girders for 2 matching material. Later work cancels on movement/lost source, not a surviving stack's count change; welding requires a lit, fueled welder.
7. Unanchor the girder with a wrench, then dismantle with a screwdriver for 2 steel. A screwdriver alone must not dismantle an anchored girder; unanchored girders must not accept anchored-only wall progression.
8. Craft metal rods at quantity greater than one. Each craft consumes 1 steel and produces 2 rods. Test held, worn-storage and nearby unobstructed inputs; depletion/full output capacity stops without loss or spill. Cancel the queue. Crafting must preserve the structure plan, and carrier inventory must not supply ingredients.
9. Repeat exposed, vertical and eligible sealed-wall disposal placement, rotation and dismantling. Verify the existing wall remains intact at sealed crossings and ordinary pipe transport still works.

These checks are acceptance evidence only after performed; this document is not a test result.
