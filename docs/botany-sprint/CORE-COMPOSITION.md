## Plant holder timing and cycle

Sources: `Content.Server/Botany/Components/PlantHolderComponent.cs` and `Content.Server/Botany/Systems/PlantHolderSystem.cs`. `UpdateDelay` is 3 seconds and `CycleDelay` is 15 seconds. The initial next poll is now plus the delay; missed cycles do not catch up in a loop. Reagent polling occurs before the growth-cycle gate, so soil chemistry still processes on polls when no growth cycle is due. Planting consumes the packet and initializes age 1, health/endurance, and last-cycle time to now. Hand planting/harvest is immediate, as are hoe and spade tool actions.

For an actual growth cycle, set last-cycle to now and perform the ordered lifecycle work:

1. Weeds roll first if water exceeds 10 and nutrition exceeds 5. Empty soil has a 0.05 chance; planted soil has a 0.01 chance. A successful roll adds `1 + WeedCoefficient` (default coefficient 1). Empty or dead plants return after the relevant weed handling.
2. Living plant age increases with probability 0.8 unless `SkipAging` is positive, in which case the skip counter decreases. Nutrition consumption independently rolls at 0.75 and subtracts nutrition rate × `SpeedMultiplier` (default 1). Water independently rolls at 0.75 and subtracts water rate × `ConsumptionMultiplier` (default 2) × speed. Health modifier is a random integer 1..2 times speed.
3. If not viable, `AffectGrowth(-1)` applies and health falls by six times the modifier. Otherwise, while `SkipAging < 10`, a plant with nutrition >5 and water >10 has a 0.35 chance of gaining modifier health; absent either resource, each deficient resource independently causes `AffectGrowth(-1)` and health loss by the modifier. `AffectGrowth` increments skip-aging below maturation. Above maturation it affects nonharvest zero-yield produce by shifting `LastProduce`; this branch is specific and should not be generalized.
4. Atmosphere conditions are then evaluated. The audited selected plants have no gas requirement. Default acceptable pressure is 81–121 kPa and ideal temperature 293 K ±10; tomato's ideal is 298 K ±10. Each failed condition reduces health by modifier. `ImproperLight` is not a requirement and must not become a guessed light/powered-growth mechanic.
5. Toxin uptake is `max(1, round(toxin / 10))`. Above tolerance (default 4), it damages health and reduces toxins. Weeds at/above tolerance (default 5) cost one health. Age beyond lifespan loses a random 3..4 health. At health <=0, mark dead, clear readiness/mutation fields, and add one weed; otherwise readiness may be set when age > production and age minus last-produce exceeds production. This readiness check has no maturation gate.
6. Clamp health to endurance, water/nutrition to 0..100, weeds to 0..10 and toxins to 0..100, then update visuals.

Appearance selection is separate from harvest eligibility: empty/dead are independent states; before maturation, stage is `max(1, int(age * GrowthStages / Maturation))`; after maturity it is the maximum stage unless ready, when the harvest appearance is used. Repeat harvest clears ready and records produce age; no-repeat removes the plant. The readiness test's production interval and the visible growth-stage rule must not be silently collapsed into one Minecraft age threshold.

The algorithm above deliberately omits upstream mutation, pest, and kudzu-only branches. It also does not claim same-seed RNG parity: local implementation must preserve audited probabilities and ordering, but should not invent an identical random-number call sequence without a compatible RNG contract.

## Metabolism and resources

The source chain establishes water's behavior directly: `Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Objects/Consumable/Drinks/water.yml` defines `Water` as a child of `BaseDrink`; `Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Objects/Consumable/Drinks/base_drink.yml` supplies inherited `PlantMetabolism` with `PlantAdjustWater` amount 1. The local `water.json` inherits local `basedrink.json`, whose metadata supplies that same effect and amount (local base drink lines 30–35). The water leaf should inherit this resolved metadata, not copy an override into `water.json` to conceal broken prototype inheritance.

At each 3-second poll, `PlantHolderSystem.UpdateReagents` invokes `ApplyEffects` only for an individual solution entry with at least 1 unit. Each applicable metabolism applies its fixed `Amount`, not an amount multiplied by reagent quantity: water therefore adds 1 water and, because the adjustment is positive, removes 4 toxin, whether the entry had 1 or 20 units. `EZNutrient` similarly gives +2 nutrition per poll. The consumer then calls `RemoveEachReagent` for the entire soil solution at rate 1 (`PlantHolderSystem.cs`, `ReagentMetabolism`/`UpdateReagents` around 881–906), so every reagent is consumed up to 1 unit each poll, including a fractional entry below 1 unit that receives no effect. This decrement is metabolized processing loss, not a container-transfer conservation claim. Use exact shared hundredth-unit arithmetic for the decrement. `PlantAdjustWater`'s implementation (`BotanySystem.cs` around 867–879) increments water by amount and detoxes by 4 × positive amount.

`PlantMetabolismData` is package-private sealed metadata with an existing codec; extend the existing dispatch/consumer under its current ownership, not a competing effects catalog. Resolved catalog tests must assert inherited effects on water through the base-drink parent chain and the runtime poll behavior, including below-one-unit no-effect consumption. Planting/seed identity association is distinct from chemical metabolism: current `EnsureUniqueSeed` retains the same seed core, so no actual mutation/changed-seed chemistry behavior is established. Implement only audited ordinary fertilizer effects. `RobustHarvest` and `PlantBGone` metadata are not justification to claim all chemistry behavior supported; harmful effects outside scope remain explicitly unsupported rather than silently applying or discarding the entire chemical system.

## Atmosphere adapter and local cell mapping

Use `LocalAtmosphereService.sample(ServerLevel, BlockPos) -> Optional<GasMixture>` for authoritative simulation. It requires a loaded valid cell and returns unknown for out-of-bounds, unloaded, or solid-owned cells; presentation `readAtmosphere` is not an authoritative substitute. `GasMixture` provides temperature in kelvin and pressure in kPa for its 1.0 m³ volume. A plant occupies a tray cell that may itself be solid, so the adaptation must verify the physical plant-occupied cell above the tray through existing geometry ownership. Unknown environment pauses the growth-cycle atmosphere decision; it is not guessed default air or vacuum. A known space sample is handled by its real pressure. No second solver or fabricated atmosphere/light default.

## Tray warning and plant-holder overlay gap

Pinned `PlantHolderSystem.OnExamine`/`UpdateSprite`, `PlantHolderVisuals`, and client `PlantHolderVisualizerSystem` establish distinct conditions: low water is <=15, low nutrition <=8, and low health is <= seed endurance/2 (no seed means no low-health warning); alerts trigger at weeds >=5, toxins >=40, improper heat/pressure, or missing required gas. Harvest overlay requires readiness. Examine always reports integer water/nutrition; its toxin warning is >40 (not the alert's >=40), and weeds >=5 are reported independently of `DrawWarnings`. `DrawWarnings` controls optional examine warnings and sprite overlays, not plant art: normal tray enables it, soil is false, and empty tray inherits true. Pest overlays are deferred. The source family `Structures/Hydroponics/overlays.rsi` (states `slowhealth3`, `lowwater3`, `lownutri3`, `alert3`, `harvest3`) is not among the 11 currently imported RSI families. Local BER plant and numeric examine exist, but warning lights and qualitative environment examine warnings do not; full `DrawWarnings` runtime parity is not implemented. Unknown local atmosphere must not be treated as definitively missing required gas. Any warning implementation needs a further biology-scope audit of `RequiresGas`; see [parity remaining gates](PARITY.md#remaining-gates).
