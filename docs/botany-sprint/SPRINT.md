## Outcome and boundary

Deliver a repeatable loop: obtain an ordinary seed packet, plant it in a real tray, care for the plant, observe growth and health, harvest produce, then either eat eligible produce or grind it into an existing compatible container. Use the required seed-extractor machine to extract seed packets from eligible produce and replant. A successful fixture must demonstrate this through normal player interactions, not direct state mutation or a public growth cheat.

Starter catalog is exactly five ordinary plants: wheat, tomato, potato, poppy, and aloe. They are SS14-style plants, not Minecraft crop-block substitutions. Required basics include trays, planting packets, numeric water/nutrition/health, growth and lifecycle, weeds and their removal, harvesting, food and grinding, and seed renewal. Do not expand this into mutations, genetics, sampling, automation, pests, special plants (including killer tomatoes), or a new assignment system. Distinguish ordinary weed pressure/removal from pest systems; defer upstream weed takeover or special spawning explicitly rather than silently importing it.

### 2. Resolved catalog, tray lifecycle, care, and tools

Implement resolved profiles and the authoritative tray/plant domain, persisted through current world conventions. Preserve normal upstream action timing; not every action becomes timed merely because a framework exists.

### 3. Harvested produce, food, and seed renewal

Harvest produces typed produce metadata: actual contents and seed association/baseline potency are distinct. Define separate eligibility/content behavior for edible and grindable outputs from audited upstream profiles. Use existing edible initialization and timed ingestion for edible items only; do not assume wheat bushels are edible without audit. Define partial-consumption policy for food that can also be ground/extracted so depleted contents cannot be refilled or duplicated. Harvest chemistry is biological generation from the seed definition, not a claim that tray water becomes reagent conservation.

Seed extraction is a required bounded machine workflow: consume eligible produce once, produce the audited packet range/profile, and reject seedless/invalid produce without mutation. Legitimate farming renewal through repeated planting, growing, harvesting, and extraction remains in scope. Extraction must not repeat on the same consumed produce, duplicate outputs, introduce a free hand-return substitute, or add genetics. Machine power, slots, placement, UI, race policy, and output ownership must use existing shared owners where justified by multiple callers; do not copy ChemMaster or introduce a platform framework prematurely.

Phases 3 and 4 may proceed in parallel only after profiles, ownership interfaces, and conservation policy are fixed.
