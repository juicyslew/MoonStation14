# Syringe manual

Hold the registered syringe in an active body hand. Use **Z** to cycle its configured draw/inject mode. Use **Alt+Z** to cycle the configured transfer dose (normally 5, 10, and 15 units).

To fill an empty syringe, aim at a loose reagent container and left-click; the syringe's mode and selected dose determine transfer direction and amount. Aim at a living body and left-click to transfer according to the syringe mode. **Shift+Z** applies the selected dose to your currently controlled body (the first-person self-target adaptation). Transfers use existing reagent holders and body metabolism; unsupported targets or policies are denied.

Steps: obtain a syringe and reagent container, fill via the loose container target, select inject mode with Z, choose a dose with Alt+Z, and left-click a living body. Verify Shift+Z while healthy to test self-use. The native carrier ghost is not a body-hand authority; use the controlled body's committed hand.
