# Medical treatment and audio corrections

`MedicalTreatmentService.complete` commits a dose before applying configured typed-damage and bleed deltas. A typed healing map may be a no-op for the patient's current injury mix while its bleed delta remains applicable (notably gauze on a bleed-only patient). The previous post-apply equality check returned `STOP` before the bleed mutation, consuming the dose without treating the patient. The check is removed: applicability is decided from both effects before consumption, and the blood mutation is still performed after the committed damage attempt.

`BloodSystem.modifyBleedAmount` treats the configured delta as an amount adjustment and now clamps the result to the resolved bloodstream policy's `[0, maxBleedRate]` range. It requires an existing initialized blood state and valid actor policy. Bleeding is not rebuilt from the damage ledger on ticks: committed positive typed injury is observed when applied, while passive decay remains the existing tick behavior. Thus an unchanged old injury cannot resurrect bleeding stopped by gauze.

Gauze policy's slash/piercing deltas, blunt exclusion, bleed amount delta, and begin/end event identifiers are resolved from the medical-item catalog; this correction does not duplicate those definitions. The event IDs are registered in `assets/moonstation14/sounds.json`, and their audio assets exist. The playback defect was recipient filtering: `ServerLevel.playSound` selects recipients from the native carrier/entity position, while this project deliberately permits the listener's active camera/body to be elsewhere. Treatment now uses shared controlled-camera dispatch: it filters connected listeners by `ServerPlayer.getCamera()` position and sends a positional event packet, then client playback rechecks active camera/world and resolves the exact registered sound ID. No vanilla broadcast is also emitted, avoiding duplicate playback. Begin is emitted only after timed interaction returns `STARTED`; end only after a dose's effect was applied. Rejection, canceled duplicate, and no-effect completion emit neither cue.

The shared dispatch reuses the grenade sound network registration path but adds a generic positional payload because the grenade payload is intentionally specific to its near/far explosion sound modes. The entity-position filtering failure mechanism is established by vanilla positional `playSound` semantics and the existing grenade implementation's explicit active-camera recipient loop; it is not a missing registration or missing sound asset.

`MedicalTreatmentGameTests.gauzeBleedOnlyAndMixedTypedEffectsStayIndependent` is an authoritative native-entity regression at the blood and damage consumer layer: it resolves a human's actual blood policy and gauze policy, applies `modifyBleedAmount` on bleed-only state, applies the resolved typed damage deltas to a mixed ledger, and exercises 60 policy-scheduled blood updates with no fresh injury. It does not simulate an authenticated player or claim to execute `MedicalTreatmentService.complete`; that real-player admission/consumption lifecycle remains outside this fixture's coverage. `MedicalItemPrototypeTest` verifies the resolved begin-sound ID; sound resources are registered separately in `sounds.json`.
