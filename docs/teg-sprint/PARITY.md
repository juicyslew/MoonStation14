## Deliberate departures / limits

Manifold presentation uses a six-face Minecraft block and vanilla iron-block placeholder. The local authoritative inventory stores one shared mixture per connected network; node-share gas is diagnostic sampling, not per-node storage. Immutable server-thread transactions validate and rollback before publish. Loaded/unloaded boundaries retain known membership and gas; unknown geometry blocks only the affected network rather than forcing chunks or inventing links. The passive connectivity stage runs independently of electrical allocations; paid processor cadence retains its canonical power-clock rules. Retired local nodes archive nonempty gas, unlike upstream storage removal, to avoid silent finite-world material loss. Upstream PipeNet pipe chemistry is not implemented locally. Full SS14 anchoring/construction, break spills, analyzer presentation and pipe chemistry are not claimed. Existing TEG equations and R6/2J1000J1200 guards remain unchanged. See [pipe-net contract](PIPE_NET_CONTRACT.md); these notes are not proof of feature-gate completion or owner acceptance.

Heater relocation audit: pinned `GasThermoMachineHeater → BaseGasThermoMachine → [BaseMachinePowered → BaseMachine → BaseStructure, ConstructibleMachine]` maps to existing local `gas_thermo_heater → gas_thermo_base → [atmos_machine_base_powered, constructible_machine_base]`. `GasThermoMachineSystem` heats any connected gas under paid power and target/tolerance hysteresis; its update does not itself invoke combustion. A continuous fuel-feed heater branch therefore needs no new composition or synthetic pipe ignition. It remains a fixture-layout choice, not an upstream-prescribed recipe; combustion continues through the finite burner owner. The original finite demonstration layout must remain unchanged.

Thermomachines do not inherit the item/pipe-base chain. `BaseGasThermoMachine` explicitly overrides `Transform.noRot` to false (`unary.yml:254–255`); ordinary static/anchored rotation is separately blocked by `RotatableSystem` unless `RotateWhileAnchored` is enabled. The relocated fixture uses existing placement-facing configuration, not a new rotation interaction.

Combustion remains on the existing finite-room reaction owner, not ordinary pipe networks. Trusted enclosed-room initialization and exchanger endpoint publication enqueue ordinary reaction work without requiring a player or an injected hotspot. The native chamber proof (`FueledChamberFoundationGameTests`, diagnostic `run_018f20e0dc55c87c170ed175`) demonstrates paid preheat, finite plasma/O₂ consumption and subsequent room-to-pipe heat with the heater off. This is not yet integrated fueled TEG/load/cooling proof. The later combined fixture must keep hot and cold working-fluid circuits separate; each pump/exchanger/circulator separates its inlet/outlet components. Turning the heater off must stop electrical heating, **not** stop chemical heat entering the hot pipe loop.

Fuel-ratio audit: pinned `PlasmaFireReaction.cs` and local `GasReactionEvaluator` use temperature scale 373.15–1643.15 K, nominal plasma burn `min(plasma, oxygen/10) * scale/9`, reagent clamping with oxygen consumption `(1.4-scale)` per plasma, and 160 kJ per plasma actually consumed. This is not fixed 1:2 O₂/plasma stoichiometry. `gases.yml` and local `GasType` give O₂ 20 and plasma 200 J/mol·K. The active continuous fixture recipe is now 6.5:1 O₂:plasma (O₂ mixer fraction 13/15) in every stage, a fixture-only response to the measured burner stock deficit; it is not a claim of optimality or guaranteed native qualification. It changes neither reaction constants nor machine physics; strict guards and native qualification remain required.

Alternate placed prototypes are not required for live layer change. Thermomachines inherit `[BaseMachinePowered, ConstructibleMachine]`, not BaseItem. Normal anchored rotation is blocked upstream; the fixed-block port does not substitute free rotation. Relevant consumers are `SharedAtmosPipeLayersSystem`, `AtmosPipeLayersSystem`, `RotatableSystem` and `AtmosUnsafeUnanchorSystem`. Live layer selection now reuses authenticated tool admission, shared timed progress and conserved relocation. Occupied destinations reject instead of upstream's overlap-triggered unanchor because the port has no unanchoring mode. Diagnostic `run_4392eb8f845f842ad3c30ae8` passed timed completion/cancellation, relocation, persistence and update-tag/decode checks; connected-client receipt remains unverified.

Control windows are limited to pressure/volume pumps, mixer/filter and heater/freezer. They consume resolved settings capabilities and bounds; mixer pressure and ratio are independently editable. Manual valves use admitted direct activation. No TEG/circulator configuration window is invented. Local drafts and correlated authoritative acknowledgements provide immediate UI feedback; this does not reproduce SS14's shared-ECS predicted setting messages or predict gas/power. Body-hand admission and exact-menu retention are Minecraft authority adaptations. In current shared nonmodal workspaces, ordinary empty-hand USE/ACTIVATE_WORLD can coexist with these windows; the server independently admits controls and separately revalidates exact-menu retention. Registered filter-menu, direct-valve and applicable readiness checks passed in diagnostic `run_94c467d11c4e919a16a3e586`; connected UI/HUD and GPU appearance remain unverified.

Trace-species observation preserves existing `GasMixture.splitRatio` cleanup below `GAS_MIN_MOLES` (5×10⁻⁸ mol) in packet and remainder. Immutable discarded-mole receipts travel through hotspot results and accepted current-cycle diagnostics. The normal finite-room pressure solver also records receipts per donor only for actual selected transfers, not scalar previews, and publishes finite-cell receipts only after a successful patch transaction into the existing scoped chunk-write observer. This adds no material or heat and changes no routing math. Fixture accounting separates whole-air/hotspot channels, excludes already committed whole-air prefixes without dropping equal-valued local events, and combines room/hotspot cleanup exactly once as nonchemical material loss. Rejected/rolled-back patches and closed/unwatched observers receive no credit. A finite vacuum is not an admitted cosmic boundary. With no finite gas heat capacity and no modeled wall-thermal reservoir, the runtime preserves packet heat while allowing ordinary pipe flow. Cosmic heat rejection requires the configured, verified exterior-space path; actual planetary ambient exchange and nonempty finite convection remain unchanged. This conservatively omits sealed-vacuum wall radiation rather than silently treating it as permanent space cooling.

Typed records represent consumed machine parameters and optional component composition, not unsupported SS14 YAML component names. Deliberate Minecraft port adaptation: APC/LV is left of facing and TEG HV is right of facing; TEG circulator gas inlet/outlet occupy front/back, leaving core adjacency clear. This avoids overlap between electrical and gas connection faces and does not require a co-host cable. Core APC demand and HV offers are both fail-closed unless the two correctly-oriented, loaded circulators resolve; role ports remain registered with zero demand/offer while invalid. Powered pressure pumps retain their configured APC receiver draw while their flow setting is disabled, matching SS14's separation of receiver load and pump flow enablement. TEG/device processor state and offers are transient per loaded host and are reset on lifecycle/power reset, not persisted. `Visual` paths remain provenance references, not delivered assets. Local gas components are rejected if streams alias, preventing double consumption; SS14 uses distinct nodes on its grid. The local cardinal facing does not model SS14 grid rotation/pipe layers.
