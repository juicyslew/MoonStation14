# TEG and atmos machinery sprint

## Goal

Deliver a usable SS14-style thermoelectric generator and the ordinary atmos machinery needed to build and operate it. Provide an understandable disposable-world fixture that proves real gas/thermal processing produces electricity delivered to a real load. Other agents own combat/hostile mobs, shuttles, and botany; those systems are outside this sprint.

## Ordered work

1. **Resolved composition and transfer foundation.** Complete the pinned upstream audit; map supported inherited behavior into typed local machine profiles. Extend existing gas inventory/topology/transaction owners for independent device ports and explicitly accounted thermal work. Do not build a second pipe graph or misuse the three pipe layers as device inlet/outlet identities.
2. **General atmos equipment.** Pressure and volume pumps, manual/digital flow valves, mixers, filters, directional pipe connections, gas heaters/freezers and passive heat exchangers. Reuse ordinary pipe routing/junctions rather than inventing a non-SS14 configurable “router.” Provide usable controls, readings, rotation and feedback through existing interaction/window owners.
3. **TEG conversion and power.** Core plus two directional circulators; separate gas streams, LV operating-power gate and HV production. Match upstream flow, thermal equations and demand/ramp behavior; report thermal generation separately from offered and actually delivered electrical power.
4. **Operating fixture.** Clearly labelled hot/cold paths, controls, startup supply, generator output and load. Integrate ordinary combustion/ignition and environmental heat exchange where existing foundations support them. A finite-charge demonstration must be labelled as such, not presented as a sustained engine. No continuous fixture gas refill, hidden heat grants, or direct power injection masquerading as generation.
5. **Validation and delivery.** Prototype inheritance/defaults and runtime-consumer tests, gas/energy accounting, device separation/rotation, authenticated controls, real allocated power and causal fixture tests. Focused cheap checks during implementation; server/client/readiness/package validation only through the serialized integration lane. No completion or owner acceptance claim from policy tests alone.

## Established SS14 constraints

Reference revision: `c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91`.

- `TegCenter` consumes 1 kW LV operating power and exports HV; two unpowered directional circulators each provide separate 100 L inlet/outlet nodes. Flow requires inlet pressure above outlet pressure.
- TEG thermal efficiency is prototype-authored `.1`, power factor `2`. Actual conversion also uses Carnot limitation and the `tanh(ΔT / 700)` taper. Offered supply has upstream demand/ramp/smoothing behavior; strict energy conservation is not an upstream claim.
- Pumps, mixers, filters, valves, thermomachines and radiators have different operation and power gates; do not apply a blanket Minecraft-style “all machines consume power” rule.
- Powered heater/freezer electrical input is distinct from pipe heat transfer. Passive radiators couple to finite local atmosphere and can radiate toward cosmic background in vacuum; empty gas itself has no cooling capacity.

Primary upstream indexes: `Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Structures/Power/Generation/teg.yml`, `Structures/Piping/Atmospherics/{binary,trinary,unary}.yml`, `Content.Server/Power/Generation/Teg/`, and atmos piping/heat-exchanger systems. Inherited defaults and supporting-device contracts are recorded in `PARITY.md`; native runtime validation remains separate from that audit.

## Acceptance proof

- Controls genuinely change live device state and flow; isolated hot/cold circuits do not silently equalize or mix through the TEG.
- Fixture readings show temperatures, pressures, operating power, thermodynamic generation, offered HV power and actual delivered energy with unambiguous units.
- Generator output powers an isolated load; startup power cannot directly reach that load. Supporting-device electrical costs are visible and accounted separately.
- Removing flow, removing LV operating power, or eliminating usable temperature difference removes generation; output settles after the documented ramp/smoothing transient. Reload/disconnection must not retain a free-power offer.
- A continuous engine claim requires a functioning fuel/heat-source and cooling path, with their costs and inventories accounted. A finite thermal discharge is useful additional evidence, not a substitute for that claim.
- SS14 sprites/sounds retain attribution; unclear provenance is recorded. Assets remain in canonical resource locations. Manual instructions stay short and suitable for an atmos beginner.
