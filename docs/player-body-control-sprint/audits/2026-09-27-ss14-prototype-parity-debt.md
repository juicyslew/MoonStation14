## Pinned reference findings

- **Human movement inheritance:** SS14 `MobHuman` inherits `BaseSpeciesMobOrganic` and `BaseMob`. The inherited `MovementSpeedModifier` defaults are acceleration 20, walk 2.5, sprint 4.5, friction 2.5, and minimum speed 0.005. Global `physics.tile_friction` is 8, making the effective local-contact friction 20. See [`species_base.yml`](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91/Resources/Prototypes/Body/species_base.yml#L62-L71), [`base.yml`](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91/Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Mobs/base.yml#L42-L56), and [`MovementSpeedModifierComponent.cs`](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91/Content.Shared/Movement/Components/MovementSpeedModifierComponent.cs#L21-L60).
- **Pig movement inheritance:** SS14 Pig inherits `SimpleMobBase`/`BaseSimpleMob` and resolves to walk 4 and sprint 4, with inherited acceleration and friction defaults. Its movement values live in the movement component/prototype inheritance, not a per-mob `movement` object. See [`animals.yml`](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91/Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Mobs/NPCs/animals.yml#L3592-L3661), [`simplemob.yml`](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91/Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Mobs/NPCs/simplemob.yml#L1-L31), and [`MovementSpeedModifierComponent.cs`](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91/Content.Shared/Movement/Components/MovementSpeedModifierComponent.cs#L21-L60).
- **Pig status and interactions:** Pig inherits the `StunImmune` tag, which causes the `CanSlip` path to reject it early; this is distinct from a `NoSlip` component. It has `StandingState` and reactive groups `Flammable`/`Extinguish` for Touch and `Acidic` for Touch plus Ingestion. The relevant stun handling is in [`movement.yml`](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91/Resources/Prototypes/Entities/StatusEffects/movement.yml#L67-L81); Pig component data is in [`animals.yml`](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91/Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Mobs/NPCs/animals.yml#L3592-L3661).
- **Slip data shape:** upstream camelCase `slipData` belongs to a reagent or a `Slippery` source, not to a mob target as a canonical prototype field. The local snake_case `slip_data` is a flattened, target-side derived field. See [`SlipperySystem.cs`](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91/Content.Shared/Slippery/SlipperySystem.cs#L92-L110) and the `Slippery` source/reagent prototypes for upstream slip data handling.

## Local divergence and boundary

- Local [`human.json`](../../../src/main/resources/data/moonstation14/moonstation14/character/human.json) and [`pig.json`](../../../src/main/resources/data/moonstation14/moonstation14/character/pig.json) duplicate movement defaults per profile. Their numeric walk/sprint values (Human 2.5/4.5; Pig 4/4) match the resolved upstream values, but the local representation does not: local data stores acceleration/friction, including effective friction 20 rather than upstream base friction 2.5. **Retain local effective friction 20 for now as explicitly approved; do not alter these values in this task.**
- Pig's local `no_slip: true` rationale does not match upstream component semantics: upstream uses inherited `StunImmune` to block ordinary slip admission, not `NoSlip`. The local `standing_eligible: false` and empty reactive groups also diverge from Pig's upstream `StandingState` and reactive methods. Human's local `reactive_methods: [touch]` omits upstream Acidic Ingestion. Local timed stun/knockdown behavior and reactive Touch behavior do exist and have GameTests; this does not establish all SS14 status/action parity, including Pig's Acidic Ingestion or generic speech, hands, and action-ownership semantics.
- Local JSON loading supports a parent but applies shallow top-level replacement: nested `slip_data` or `movement` objects are replaced as a whole. It does not consume SS14 component-aware inherited YAML directly. A future importer must resolve pinned YAML inheritance and defaults, then adapt explicitly to supported Minecraft behavior; it must fail on unknown/unsupported fields rather than silently copying or dropping them.
- `host_entity_types` is a local bridge from Minecraft `EntityType` registry IDs to character prototypes for reused vanilla hosts, not upstream SS14 prototype data. A custom mob still needs a prototype/character binding, but that binding need not be duplicated in character JSON if spawn or registration establishes prototype identity. Current autojoin still requires a host entry unless another explicit binder is implemented. Since one entity type can have multiple character profiles, host mapping alone cannot select the per-instance profile; a future design needs per-instance binding.
- `thermal` is an aggregate local field under concurrent Atmos ownership. Its values and provenance are explicitly out of scope for this pass and remain pending independent provenance; do not change or assess thermal data here.
