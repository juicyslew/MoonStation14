## Ordered stages

1. **Hotspot exposure policy and one-shot reaction — implemented, tested for covered cases.** Pure exposure decisions use the local `Default` 800 K metadata and intensity; transient reaction state does not directly warm an entire gas cell, heat puddles, or place fire blocks. Keep atmosphere default OFF. Focused JUnit and the executed finite cold-gas fixture cover no immediate gas/energy mutation and a later due-step reaction without heating the whole cell to 800 K. Focused queue tests cover retained transient exposure under backlog and eventual TTL expiry; the server fixture does not establish broader no-op/puddle behavior or full stress acceptance.
2. **Loaded-cell server bridge and due burn steps — implemented, tested for covered cases; burst liveness open.** The executed fixture covers a loaded finite-claimed cell and due burn; other exercised atmosphere fixtures and focused tests cover their own cases. Never force-load touched cells. Preserve the existing 60-tick fuse and N/M hand path. Pending blast exposures have capacity 256 and remain eligible for retries for up to 64 due steps rather than expiring after the first due step. Focused queue tests cover a 256-cell round-robin backlog consumed over up to 32 due steps, retry and eventual TTL expiry, quench, chunk unload, and repeated-offer merge without postponement. More than 256 distinct pending cells can still be rejected at admission, and `ServerExplosionService` does not surface that false return; competing work may outlast the TTL, while nonviable exposures may retry until expiry. This is **not** guaranteed safe deferral or full stress acceptance; burst-liveness/rejection behavior remains open.
3. **Knockback policy and application — implemented for living entities, tested for covered cases.** Pure outward direction/velocity is capped, not a literal 10 blocks/tick translation; the server blast service applies one impulse per eligible living-entity hit. Focused policy JUnit and executed GameTests cover bounded outward living knockback, ineligible targets unchanged, and rejected blasts without knockback. This does not claim all SS14 movable entities are supported or owner-client feel acceptance.
4. **Volatile grenade chain reactions — pure thresholds only; gameplay integration outstanding.** `VolatileGrenadeDamage` covers 10/45 edges in focused JUnit, but is not connected to damage, held/world/projectile grenade ownership, activation, or fuse state. First define a single owner for each identity/lifecycle and wire the damage event to the existing fuse/activation path; then add a bounded deferred chain queue with epoch/idempotence guards, never recursive same-tick processing. At >=10 start the configured fuse only if not already running; at >=45 activate immediately, subject to upstream behavior and idempotency. Preserve `ExGrenade`'s 3-second/60-tick fuse; do not add impact/proximity/autodetonation. **Accept:** executed server tests for threshold edges, identity transfer, queue caps/overflow, duplicate suppression, and fuse preservation.
5. **Connected integration and owner acceptance — outstanding.** The latest isolated server suite (`-03`) passed 371 required tests after the queue fix, including the hotspot and knockback fixtures, but chain order is not implemented/tested in gameplay and the owner connected-client smoke for transient visibility and knockback feel is still outstanding. **Accept:** record actual server-world results and manual answers below, including failures/deferred work; fixture results alone do not close this stage.

## Hazards and boundaries

- Hotspot/reaction work is transient; distinguish its restart/expiry behavior from persisted gas and finite claims in server-world tests.
- Any gas-energy accounting requires an explicit debit/ledger. Exposure temperature is not permission to heat an entire cell.
- Cap both per-tick work and chain queue size; overflow must not recurse or overload the server. Exposure capacity is 256 and TTL is 64 due steps: over-cap admission is rejected (not surfaced by `ServerExplosionService`), competing work can exceed TTL, and nonviable work can retry until TTL. Focused backlog/expiry tests do not resolve full burst-liveness acceptance. Chain queue behavior is not implemented yet.
- Gameplay effects remain server authoritative; clients may present replicated results only.
- TNT and other explosives are out of scope unless explicitly planned and accepted.
- The old sprint's owner-manual caveats and unrelated GameTest failure remain separate from this sprint's executed fixture results; neither is evidence of new-effects acceptance.

## Manual acceptance prompts (when applicable)

- Does a blast produce only the intended transient hotspot/exposure, with no unintended gas warming or puddle heating?
- Does knockback feel bounded and outward without launching entities across the map?
- Do damage-triggered grenades respect the timer, including held/world/projectile cases, without same-tick recursive chains?
- Do disabled-atmosphere and unloaded-cell cases remain inert, and does the existing 60-tick grenade fuse/hand path still work?
