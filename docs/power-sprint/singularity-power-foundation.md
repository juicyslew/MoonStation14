# Singularity-sprint power foundation boundary

The pure HV allocator now supports a supplier offer with an explicit finite rated maximum and an independently sampled available-watts value. This maps to upstream `PowerSupplier.MaxSupply` and its current supply, respectively; the compatibility four-argument `PowerLiveLoop.Source` remains the legacy 30 kW-clipped adapter. The live fixed generator remains 25 kW. Downstream substation transfer/efficiency and APC limits are unchanged.

Verified upstream checkout revision: `c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91`. The Singularity collector declares `PowerSupplier` and its computed maximum supply is not a fixed 30 kW contract. The PA power box and emitter are `PowerConsumer` devices on MV, not APC-tier loads; PA power-box standby/on values are 500/2000/3500/5000/6500 W, and emitter is 600 W on / 1 W off. No upstream or local implementation is claimed here for singularity, PA, or emitter gameplay.

The pure `PowerLiveLoop.MvConsumer(id, component, known, demandWatts)` contract is available, and the loaded-world bridge now exposes `PowerRuntimeNode` for future block entities. A node declares its exact `DevicePort`s, optional `SupplierOffer(currentWatts, ratedMaximumWatts)` or optional MV demand, and receives actual solve allocation. Both current offer and rated maximum accept finite nonnegative values, including `(0,0)` for a genuinely inactive supplier; positive offers are bounded by rated maximum in `PowerAllocation`. The legacy source adapter rejects nonfinite/negative values before applying its historical 30 kW clip. The live loop discovers such entities through the existing `DEVICES` lifecycle index/chunk-load path, resolves declared ports through `PowerGraphService`, snapshots offers once per solve, and submits supplier offers and MV demands to the existing `PowerLiveLoop` together. A BE declaring both supplier and MV-consumer roles is rejected fail-closed for both roles (including zero-output supplier offers): it creates no source offer, receives no MV grant, and reports unknown meter status. Legacy `PowerDeviceBlockEntity` roles continue unchanged and cannot also be counted as a generic source. Meter observations are tied to the same loaded BE identity, demand snapshot, topology generation, and fresh solve sample; identity replacement, unload, removal, unknown graph, or changed demand denies stale observations. No chunk is forced and no additional world tick or registry scan was added.

This is an integration seam, not production machine wiring: no resolved collector supplier component or PA/emitter prototype consumer yet implements `PowerRuntimeNode`; the focused GameTest uses a test-only subclass on existing registered power-device block entity types and real cable graph ports. No generator fuel/radiation behavior, full prototype inheritance consumer path, gameplay machine behavior, or full 3D containment is claimed. The fixed live source remains 25 kW and retains the legacy four-argument offer path.
