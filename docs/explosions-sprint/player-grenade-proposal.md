# Player-triggered grenade

## Status and scope

**Status: implementation present and automated behavior verified; connected-client acceptance and balance approval remain outstanding.** A fixed, disposable player grenade has a controlled-body prime/throw path and a timed explosion. Modular grenades remain deferred.

The project is greenfield: there is no production server or save migration. Structural station blocks are intentionally destructible by this grenade. Claims/world-protection are future concerns if multiplayer or claims are introduced; they are not current blockers, and this slice has no operator-only or disposable-world gate. There is no generic configuration, operator, or dimension gate.

## SS14 reference facts

These facts are interaction/lifecycle references from audited SS14 checkout `c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91`, not local balance approval:

- `ExGrenade` primes on use; throwing is a separate action. Its fuse is three seconds / 60 ticks. It beeps at tick 0 and tick 40, then expires at tick 60.
- Pinned `ExGrenade` source `Explosive` values are total intensity 120 / slope 3 / maximum intensity 10. Pinned SS14 `Default` explosion metadata separately specifies damage (heat/blunt/piercing 5 each), break curve/reroll metadata, and temperature 800 K; it does not specify `fireStacks` (the separate `FireBomb` does specify `fireStacks: 2`). These are prototype-sourced facts, not local effects or parity requirements.
- The local registered/server-resolved `moonstation14:ex_grenade` source prototype at `data/moonstation14/moonstation14/explosive_source/ex_grenade.json` currently contains provisional 380/3/10 and selects `explosion_type: default`; unknown/unresolved source prototypes fail closed. Its 380 intensity is a local provisional gameplay adjustment, not SS14 parity or validated balance. The local `moonstation14:default` explosion prototype at `data/moonstation14/moonstation14/explosion/default.json` contains damage/break metadata and `temperature_kelvin: 800`, but local gas hotspot creation, heat deposition, and fire-stack application are not implemented. Do not infer SS14 thermal behavior from metadata presence.
- Modular casing/trigger/payload construction is deferred.

## Current interaction and implementation boundaries

- Temporarily configurable `N` primes the grenade in the active body hand; `M` throws it. These keys are temporary pending a future context-aware input system (for example E/Z/left-click). A generic throw system after the hands/inventory/interactions sprints is explicitly **out of scope**.
- There is no generic item-use/throw dispatcher for this path. Requests use controlled-body authority through `LIVE_HANDS`; the vanilla carrier and `JugItem.use` are forbidden for player grenade behavior. The player primes while holding the item. Throw transfers the already-armed grenade and preserves its original fuse deadline. Server-authoritative impacts against walls/floors have damped bounce/slide/friction; impact neither detonates nor resets the 60-tick fuse, and only fuse expiry blasts. If held through expiry, it detonates in hand. If the grenade's block position is occupied, the trusted origin resolver uses an adjacent loaded AIR voxel as the blast origin; it does not move the grenade entity or load chunks.
- The creative-tab item uses vanilla `minecraft:block/cocoa_stage2` geometry via `assets/moonstation14/models/item/grenade.json`, with item display transforms for the hand and a raised ground transform to lift the pod visually. The thrown entity still uses vanilla `ThrownItemRenderer`; server physics are unchanged. Its registered, attributed SS14 `beep1.ogg` plays on PRIME at 0 and again at +40 ticks, and registered `explosion1.ogg` plays when the blast is applied. These match the exact prebuilt `ExGrenade` three-second fuse / two-beep cadence. Both fixed sound events use packet radius 32; each sample has `attenuation_distance: 32` in `sounds.json`. Call-site gains are 0.35 for the beep and 0.5 for the explosion; pitch is unchanged. Original SS14 `beep1.ogg` and `explosion1.ogg` are unchanged MONO samples; original SS14 `explosionfar.ogg` is an unchanged STEREO sample imported with its CC-BY-SA-3.0 notice retained. For applied/partial blasts, same-dimension real players selected server-side with `getCamera()` receive exactly one mode: positional near audio at <=32, or far audio in (32,96). Far plays locally at the camera with bounded gain <=0.25 tapered toward zero at 96, not as a global broadcast. No audibility beyond 96 or around the carrier is claimed. Carrier/camera movement and OpenAL perception remain unverified in a connected client. This is distinct from modular/configurable `TimerTrigger`, whose pinned prototype defaults to a five-second timer, one-second beep interval, and `general_beep.ogg` ([prototype](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91/Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Objects/Devices/Electronics/triggers.yml#L12-L38), [component](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91/Content.Shared/Trigger/Components/TimerTriggerComponent.cs#L74)). After an applied or partial blast, the local Minecraft translation also emits bounded, brief vanilla `EXPLOSION` (1), `FLAME` (16), and `LARGE_SMOKE` (12) particles. This is not SS14's `Default` tile-based `fire.rsi` overlay, light, or camera shake (pinned [`explosion.yml`](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91/Resources/Prototypes/explosion.yml#L11-L13), [`ExplosionOverlay.cs`](https://github.com/space-wizards/space-station-14/blob/c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91/Content.Client/Explosion/ExplosionOverlay.cs#L103-L119)). The throwable's throw sound remains a vanilla placeholder. Retain the adjacent audio license notice and see [grenade audio attribution](grenade-audio-attribution.md) for provenance and redistribution terms.
- A retained M press during the PRIME result/full snapshot refresh is handled; server-side strict token and revision checks are unchanged. Timer beeps do not change the ownership revision.
- The bounded blast classifies ordinary non-air blocks with nonnegative destroy speed at fixed local tolerance 2, including dirt, grass, stone, chests, torches, rails, plants, and station floors/walls/ceilings. Negative-hardness blocks and explicit admin/special blocks (including bedrock, barriers, command/structure blocks, and portals) remain protected. The blast is capped and never loads chunks; planner safety `MAX_VISITED=256` is a hardcoded implementation bound, not a prototype-sourced SS14 value. Local tolerance 2 is also a hardcoded implementation choice. Applying setBlock-to-AIR may delete block-entity/container contents; no drops are guaranteed. Local 380/3/10 and tolerance 2 are provisional and not validated balance or SS14 parity; the pinned SS14 source remains 120/3/10.
- No chunk loading is performed to complete grenade effects. The fuse currently ticks only while the authoritative living controlled body is connected; a disconnected/dead body does not tick its held fuse. This limitation is not a promise of offline expiry or save/reload lifecycle behavior.

## Manual test checklist

1. Enable controlled-body experimental control if necessary. Obtain the grenade through existing pickup and place it in the body hand; a creative give of the carrier does not authorize body-hand use.
2. Press **N**, then **M** promptly; also test N+M in the same tick and M after +2 seconds. Verify retained M during PRIME result/full snapshot refresh and confirm server strict token/revision checks reject stale or invalid actions. Beeps must not change ownership revision.
3. Confirm bounded destruction of ordinary dirt, stone, and grass as well as mod structural blocks; confirm bedrock and barrier stay protected. On a connected client, verify visual cue appears only upon successful fuse detonation with an applied/partial blast.
4. Test bounded bounce, slide, and stopping, and verify the fuse continues and expires while the grenade rests on the ground. Test floor, wall, and ceiling impacts; visually inspect rendered pod in hand and as a projectile resting after bounce for ground clipping.
5. With connected clients, check near positional cues at <=32 and the bounded far cue for (32,96), including camera-aware selection with a far carrier and moving camera. Do not infer audibility beyond 96 or around the carrier. These connected-client checks remain unverified.
6. Confirm unloaded boundaries do not trigger chunk loading or cause out-of-bounds effects. These checks do not require obsolete operator/dimension settings; none exist.

These steps are manual acceptance guidance, not a claim that a connected client has been tested. After prototype migration, coordinator validation ran ` .\gradlew.bat compileJava compileGameTestJava test --rerun-tasks --no-daemon` successfully: 1,752 JUnit tests, zero failures. A fresh isolated `build/gametest-grenade-source-prototype-20261001-01` run completed 368 GameTests; the single required unrelated failure was `leasedpigandvillagerkeepvanillawalkanimation`. No grenade, explosion, or pickup test failed; the full GameTest suite was NOT green. Camera/carrier movement and OpenAL sound perception remain unverified in a connected client; presentation, particles, and animation/interpolation also remain untested. Client bounce/overlap/audio acceptance and numeric balance approval remain outstanding; no in-game validation is claimed here.

For arithmetic context only, an all-open pure-kernel case at local 380/3/10 completes 129 affected/visited cells, with Manhattan extent 4 and axis extent 4. The outer-axis intensity is approximately 0.060606, below ordinary solid tolerance 2. This does not establish a four-block destruction radius. SS14's roughly four-tile 2D prototype behavior is an approximate observation from a different algorithm, not parity or validated balance. Local Default temperature is metadata only: gas hotspot creation/heat deposition and ignition/fire-stack effects are not implemented or promised; atmosphere topology integration remains conditional on atmos being enabled.
