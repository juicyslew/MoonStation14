# Visual retest

Use the rebuilt jar on both client and server.

1. Place powered two-block airlocks facing each direction. Check the closed model,
   inventory/held item, front/rear reader and absence of geometry outside the doorway.
2. Open and close them. Upper/lower leaves should move vertically, clear the
   doorway at passability, and never leave floating reader hardware or stretched UVs.
3. Check unpowered, denied and bolted presentation; toggle bolt lights and open the
   maintenance panel. Base paint should not glow; powered overlays should.
4. Join/reload while a door is open. It should stay visually open without replaying
   its animation. Check examination outlines in both poses.

External storage pockets are intentionally omitted. One-high doors and spray
painting are not implemented by this mini-sprint.
