# ChemMaster output client check

Connected acceptance is complete; the owner closed the sprint. These steps remain available for regression checks. Build a fresh fixture and use normal pickup and interaction controls:

1. Run `/ms14 chemmaster_fixture build [origin]`. Confirm the loaded, clear 8x10 footprint and check status for the actual 250 W power meter. Collect the staged filled water/sugar beaker, bottle, and pill canister; bare canisters are valid empty canisters.
2. Hold the beaker and use it on the ChemMaster (INPUT slot). Hold the canister and use it on the machine (OUTPUT slot), then open the machine with **E** while hands are held. Unlock the cursor with the existing mouse-control toggle.
3. Select BUFFER or INPUT as the source. Choose dose **20u** and a palette appearance separately (the palette has 20 appearances); set count and shared label. Check label selection, Ctrl+A, paste, caret editing, fit, and Escape. Escape should release field focus without stealing world clicks, movement, chat, or cursor control. Source refresh should preserve the label; reagent transfer may regenerate it.
4. Produce pills FROM BUFFER, then switch to INPUT and produce more. Verify contents and remaining cells. Use **Z** on the held canister to open its storage, retrieve a pill, then use **Z** on the whole pill. After 12 ticks exactly one pill is consumed with the pill-use cue, not drink behavior. Cancel a pill action and confirm contents are unchanged. Fill the bottle to its 30u cap, label and eject it; drinking from the held bottle remains the existing 5u adaptation.
5. Try full and insufficient requests; controls should disable or reject without changing contents. Drag and resize at representative GUI scales; output must not clip or overlap, and no `Pending` state should appear.
   - Final retest: choose count 2 and dose 5, then edit the label and change appearance; count and dose should remain 2 and 5. Produce pills: the explicit dose must remain 5 while count updates for remaining source/space. Press **Z** again on the same held canister to close its storage. Confirm uniform palette buttons and one artwork pixel scale (smaller rounds are intentional). Held bags/belts also open storage on **Z**; equip them through inventory or smart-equip instead. A completed pill use without stomach room should show “You can't swallow any more!” and retain the pill.
