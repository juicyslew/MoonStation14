# Botany fixture: operator guide

Requires an authorized operator with permission 2. Run `/ms14 botany_fixture` in a fully loaded, empty area. The fixture refuses occupied space; it does not clear arbitrary entities or change owner configuration. It builds three rooms containing 52 numbered bays, moves your authorized body, and supplies a kit. Machines are powered; torches provide visibility.

Enter at bay 01 and follow the signed serpentine route in numeric order: 01–18, 19–40, then 41–52. Each bay has one plant, one front sign, and one torch. Use ordinary Shift-left-click examination within three blocks and Shift-scroll to cycle the target. Follow the sign for the bay's demonstration. Ordinary interaction feedback—not a counter—is the proof: plants can be dead, growing, or ready; actions can show warnings, screams, and produce/contact results.

Try the actual in-world actions: harvest, hoe, water, use a beaker, sample with the kit wirecutter, and grind/extract real produce using the powered machines. Do not infer an outcome from a preset label. Baseline bays 01 and 02 are natural-mutation trial choices; dose normally, allow ordinary processing, and examine again. Mutation is stochastic: an unchanged trial is valid. Optional developer inspection may be used before/after capture, but is not normal gameplay proof. A preset demonstrates a starting trait, never mutation acquisition.

For mutation demonstrations and limitations, use the active [genetics manual](botany-genetics-sprint/MANUAL.md) and [showcase ledger](botany-genetics-sprint/SHOWCASE-COVERAGE.md); both use numeric bay identifiers. Avoid relying on older lettered station names or duplicated station descriptions here. `/ms14 botany_fixture status` is optional developer/technical output, not the main flow or ordinary proof.

Sentient plants and slippery-plant contact are unsupported. Do not claim firearm, mob, or instrument interactions. The fixture is a bounded demonstration, not a complete species or mutation showcase; visual review and owner acceptance are separate.
