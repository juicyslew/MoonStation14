# Botany operator fixture

Requires a registered character with permission level 2. In addition to the room area, leave the external utility footprint x5..9, y0..3, z-4..-1 clear, loaded, and in bounds. The entire build footprint must be loaded, empty, and in bounds. Run the unchanged single setup command; it builds the room and power network, initializes finite air, moves the existing authorized body into the room, grants the kit, and arranges scenarios after normal tray initialization ticks:

`/ms14 botany_fixture`

`/ms14 botany_fixture status` reports room atmosphere and power state. Setup creates an 11×6×5 room with 106 finite-air cells, within the trusted 128-cell setup budget. Initialization explicitly seeds the finite air; it does not use ambient fallback. The kit uses resolved seed/reagent prototypes and `BodyGiveService`, placing supplies in hands first and dropping the remainder into the room. Setup and status are permission-gated. Failures may leave a partial room and report that fact; no automatic destructive rollback is performed.

Scenarios arrange five trays: repeat-ready tomato, ready wheat, empty/weedy with low water and nutrition, planted low-health, and an untouched normal tray for ordinary growth observation. Ready scenarios advance by bounded normal metabolism ticks; this is not a free-running accelerated/public growth command. Examine trays (Shift-click) for identity/resources; after successful same-block use, examine again to see refreshed status.
