# Finite gas pressure release foundation

`GasPressureRelease.release` is a detached, pure finite-mixture calculation reused by live `GasCanisterState.refillFromInsertedTank`; it remains the single finite pressure-transfer algorithm. The state validates its state/slot/ledger revisions and active slot claim, prepares both resulting mixtures and a `GasTankItem.withMixture` payload, commits through existing `MachineItemSlot.mutate`, then publishes canister gas/revision only after slot mutation succeeds. The in-place mutation keeps the tank token and ledger claim and preserves unrelated stack components. It reuses `GasMixture`'s ideal-gas pressure, validated inventory, species heat capacities, removal, and gas-energy adjustment APIs. Volumes remain those of their respective source and receiver.

The pressure gate and half-pressure-difference cap follow pinned SS14 `Content.Server/Atmos/EntitySystems/AtmosphereSystem.Gases.cs:144-166` (`ReleaseGasTo`), revision `c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91`. `GasCanisterSystem.DeviceUpdated` (`Content.Server/Atmos/Piping/Unary/EntitySystems/GasCanisterSystem.cs:90+`) receives `AtmosDeviceUpdateEvent` created with `RealAtmosTime()` in `Content.Server/Atmos/EntitySystems/AtmosphereSystem.Processing.cs:609`; `RealAtmosTime()` is the number of enabled atmosphere processing states times `AtmosTime`, and device work may be deferred when the processing deadline is reached. Local `GasCanisterEntity` instead transfers at most once every 20 entity ticks (nominal one second), a coarse cadence adaptation to prevent the helper's per-call pressure cap from running 20 times per second. This is not exact cadence parity or dt-scaled flow. Only inserted-tank equalization is implemented: no world-gas receiver means no transfer; world venting, UI controls, reactions and byproducts remain deferred. Local profiles govern target bounds; SS14's base output target is 101.325 kPa, with canister prototype max overridden to 1013.25 kPa.
