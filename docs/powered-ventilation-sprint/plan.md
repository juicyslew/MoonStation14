## Goal and exact scope

Deliver a powered, passive (automatic once configured) 3D gas network joining atmosphere, combustion, and power simulations, plus player-installable exposed gas-pipe segments, a one-clicked-block sealed straight wall segment, and a genuinely handheld T-ray scanner. Vent/scrubber room-facing devices are distinct devices/items; they are not pipes and do not automatically open walls. Finite gas moves between finite rooms through finite network storage; scrubber capture is separately accounted; no gas moves while unpowered. Network connectivity/storage and room `AtmosphereTopology` remain separate.

Exposed gas pipes are freely 3D on walls, ceilings, maintenance areas, and exterior surfaces, with bends and vertical runs. Their segment item is the installer. Sneak-use targets exactly one reachable clicked full-solid, atmos-sealing wall block to install one straight sealed segment. Validate server-side the exact clicked face/position, reach, sneak state, one-block scope, protection/permissions, and held corresponding segment item. No additional tool, remote/far-side placement, auto-traversal, or continuation suggestion. The host remains unchanged full-collision and AtmosphereTopology-sealing; pipe installation never creates AIR, even transiently, removes the host, alters topology, or opens room gas. Unsupported/uncertain hosts fail closed. Incomplete ports are capped. A connected crossing is a gas-network edge, not an air route.

Thick-wall construction is manual: physically expose deeper positions, then rebuild and install/fill segments farthest-to-nearest. Demolition is separate and may expose air; only actual destruction of the final seal can breach a room. Removal interaction/tool, side/access, cost, and granularity are open M0 decisions; do not invent a removal tool or cost. No forced chunk loads. Use sparse per-host records, explicit identity/incarnation invalidation so same-type host replacement never inherits a segment, and loaded-only bounded topology.

The scanner is genuinely handheld and observer-specific. The player-installed segment item is usable by a controlled character body only, through its exact settled live hand/session; never a spectator carrier, and there is no free/uncommitted-player installation path this sprint. While validly activated by that controlled character, it reveals concealed gas pipes AND electrical cables within a bounded true 3D sphere, including across floors. It is not general x-ray and grants no access, mutation, or removal. No client-authored target/radius and no scan-induced change to ordinary visibility. The scanner reveal payload is targeted only to its active observer, never globally broadcast. Cable concealment is presentation-only for now: existing ordinary concealed cable snapshots/resync may expose coordinates to modified clients. This is not confidentiality or cheating prevention; server-filtering redesign is deferred follow-up. Preserve gas hidden-data minimization as a design goal, but separately document whether ordinary gas base packets withhold or expose concealed coordinates; do not claim visual-only concealment secures gas data against modified clients. Tests must distinguish the targeted additional scanner reveal from ordinary cable records a non-scanning client may already receive.

Disposal tubes/entity transport, disposal segment items, and any cross-type equal semantics are excluded, and may be considered only in a later independent “Disposals” sprint with separate ownership, compatibility, capacity, geometry, and transport design. No explosions, infinite sinks, or unrelated cleanup.
