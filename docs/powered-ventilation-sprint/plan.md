# Powered ventilation sprint plan

**Status: proposed next sprint; planning only. This document does not authorize implementation.** MoonStation14 does not currently have powered gas ducts, vents, or scrubbers. Obtain explicit project-owner and atmosphere/power-owner approval before any code, prototype, or asset work. Explosions are owned by another agent and are out of scope; this proposal makes no explosion coupling decisions.

## Goal and scope boundary

Plan a system-heavy, passive (automatic once configured), powered 3D gas ventilation network joining the existing atmosphere, combustion, and power simulations. It should move finite gas between finite rooms through a finite sealed gas store, permit separately accounted scrubber capture, and stop moving gas when unpowered. Runtime behavior requires no character commands, UI, item, inventory, placement interaction, or player configuration. Developer-created fixtures may preplace devices/ducts for tests. This is not the implementation sprint authorization.

The product direction is exposed, genuinely 3D six-face gas ducting: horizontal bends and vertical risers; graph connections are distinct from room connections. Ducts have capped, incomplete ends; networks and every storage volume are finite. Room-facing intakes and releases are separately oriented devices, not open duct-to-room routes. Scrubbers move selected species into a separate finite waste branch/store; they do not destroy it. Power-off means no gas transit, not merely a dark indicator. Network connectivity/storage and room `AtmosphereTopology` remain separate; duct or device state never makes a solid host passable, opens a wall, or merges rooms.

This is a deliberately narrow preplaced/test-fixture system. No player construction, placement, wall crossing, host mutation, block interaction, scanner, hidden-wall state, privacy implementation, inventory/equipment, disposal transport, or explosion integration is included. Do not create a vent outlet to immutable exterior as the first milestone, or an infinite waste sink. A future room-facing transfer must use atmos-owner-approved transaction semantics. Gas and disposal remain distinct systems.

## Recommended direction (owner decisions remain open)

**RECOMMENDED:** a finite six-face sealed duct graph with per-component finite volume and gas store, mutation-driven loaded-only topology, and compatible capped endpoints. Exposed ducts occupy air/passable cells. Any proposed duct fixture on an existing full-solid wall host must preserve that cell's actual `AtmosphereTopology` passability; do not imply a full-solid block at every duct position or retrofit/seal walls this sprint. M0 chooses an implementable geometry/representation and verifies host passability before and after fixture placement. Keep finite storage physically explicit at graph/component level rather than inventing per-tick transfer at every segment. A separated room intake and release transfer through the network, with scrubber capture into its own finite connected store/branch. Begin with two known finite rooms; unknown, exterior, and provisional boundaries fail closed. Use small developer fixtures/configuration only. No full-world scans, forced chunk loads, or per-segment ticking.

**OWNER DECISION at M0:** whether stored duct gas reacts chemically. Do not assume inert gas or SS14 parity. Decide with atmosphere/reaction owner before M1 gas semantics are fixed.

At M0 also settle endpoint orientation/validation, transfer direction, outlet target policy, scrubber filters (including whether SS14's cited default is appropriate), capture back-pressure/full-store behavior, pressure targets, flow rates, network/volume/storage capacities, power in watts and allocation cadence, failure and restart semantics, and observational/debug visibility. Mark proposals as recommendations, never silently convert them into settled values. No fabricated numerical values or exact SS14 parity.

Shared per-component network gas requires an explicit persistence and volume owner, stable component identity/generation, and exact deterministic gas/enthalpy partition rules on split and partial chunk unload. Define rejoin/reload reconciliation so it cannot duplicate stored gas, and bound any unresolved frontier/work. These are M0 prerequisites and an M1 gate, not implementation details to defer. M0 must also choose one power billing contract: either solver-prepaid interval energy or per-step joule escrow, with exactly one bill (never both); specify grant expiry and immediate invalidation on breaker or topology change. Tests must assert total delivered joules do not exceed solver-paid joules, including partial grants and mid-interval shutdown.

## End-to-end acceptance scenario

Use only test/developer fixture creation (no character commands): two separately finite rooms at different heights, separated by solid walls, connected by an exposed 3D duct route including a vertical riser and a chunk seam. Exposed ducts use air/passable cells; any fixture on an existing full-solid wall host preserves its actual passability. Probe wall and air `AtmosphereTopology` ownership/passability before and after placement. No full-solid blocking is implied for every duct position and there is no sealed-wall retrofit. Create/seed controlled tritium fuel or combustion products in the source room, then measure actual fuel before depletion: reaction consumes tritium into water vapor; it does not generate tritium. Intentionally transfer gas through the finite duct system to the destination room. Capture a selected scrubbed species into a distinct finite waste branch and account for it there. Put a lamp and ventilation device on shared APC capacity and prove actual contention. Breaker-off and battery exhaustion stop transfer; restoration allows it to resume from fresh topology/power state. Save/reload preserves gas ledger within declared numerical tolerance. Unknown/provisional/exterior destinations, capped/broken ducts, full storage, and unloaded frontiers do not exchange gas. No explosion behavior is exercised or specified. Acceptance reports measured moles moved/captured in a stated interval, zero transfer while off, species/energy error, per-level caps/probes/synchronization, and benchmark against M0-set targets (no invented numeric ms SLA).

2. **Power:** Device W, allocation cadence, priority/fairness against lamps, behavior on partial grant, battery policy, and choose exactly one solver-prepaid interval or per-step joule escrow contract, grant expiry and immediate breaker/topology invalidation at a 2-tick gas step versus 20-tick solve. No double billing; delivered joules cannot exceed solver-paid joules.
3. **Chemistry:** Are ducts inert, or do approved reactions run in the network? What thermal treatment, reaction ordering, persistence and explosion-owner interface boundary apply? No explosion coupling in this scope.
4. **Transactions/recovery:** Atmosphere API for two finite rooms and finite pipe store; separate chemistry deltas from TRANSFER-only species/enthalpy equalities; all-participant preflight before writes, ordered commit with rollback of prior writes on known failure before publication, and a reviewed recovery/journal design where rollback cannot be guaranteed; crash/chunk persistence ordering?
5. **Topology/lifecycle:** Component identity/generation and persistence/volume owner; exact deterministic gas partition on split/partial unload and duplicate-free rejoin/reload; loaded frontier behavior; bounded mutation/discovery/probe/packet budgets; supported preplacement fixture mechanism and server authority?
6. **Endpoints:** Intake/release orientation, allowable outlet target, finite room check, direction, back-pressure and failure behavior? Exterior outlet explicitly not first milestone.
7. **Scrubbing:** Which species and rates, filtered ordering, capture capacity/back-pressure and waste-branch topology? SS14 default filter list is reference evidence only, not an approved local default.
8. **Observability/presentation:** Minimum diagnostic data and client visibility; who approves network state exposure? No UI/character command needed for normal operation; no scanner/hidden state.
9. **Ownership:** Confirm atmosphere, power, persistence, diagnostics, fixtures, visuals, and acceptance owners; explosions explicitly remain with their other agent.
