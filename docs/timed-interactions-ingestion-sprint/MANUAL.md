# Connected check

Restart both client and server for the new protocol/catalog. Control a body with
a filled bottle/jug and access to a puddle.

1. Press Z: visible progress precedes each drink; press Z again to stop. Switch
   hands while retaining the source, then drop/store it: only the latter cancels.
2. Use Alt+E or Alt+left-click on a puddle with an empty hand: each dose takes
   three seconds. Repeat the same gesture to stop. Check last-dose cleanup.
3. Check the self bar low in first person (above the hotbar) and above the head in third person; have
   a second player observe progress. Damage interrupts; ordinary movement does not.
4. Open storage during drinking: normal window/ownership behavior remains intact;
   completed doses update fill icons promptly. No extra dose occurs after cancellation.
