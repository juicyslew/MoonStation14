## Armor parity decision

Verified upstream revision: `c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91`. `SharedArmorSystem.cs:46–52`, `InventorySystem.Relay.cs:117–131`, `DamageSpecifier.cs:126–155` and `FixedPoint2.cs:53–60,96–109` establish sequential per-item flat-before-coefficient application with hundredth precision after each item. Thus 10 radiation with coefficients 0.01 and 0.65 becomes 0.10 then **0.06**, not the incoming float helper's 0.065. Generic coefficients above one are valid vulnerabilities (`DamageModifierSet.cs:9–26`). The merged shared kernel preserves these rules rather than maintaining competing armor implementations.
