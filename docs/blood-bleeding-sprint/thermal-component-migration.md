# Thermal mob-component migration

The old top-level `thermal` map is rejected (including `null` and abstract prototypes). Move its 19 fields into three typed entries in `components`; omitted entries do **not** acquire defaults. Component fragments inherit and patch individual fields by type, using the normal character component merge strategy. A resolved `TemperatureDamage` or `ThermalRegulator` without `Temperature` is invalid. When damage is present, both the initial `current_kelvin` and any regulator setpoint must lie strictly between the cold and heat damage thresholds.

| Component | Fields | Ownership |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Temperature` | `mass_kg`, `specific_heat_joules_per_kg_kelvin`, `atmosphere_transfer_efficiency`, `current_kelvin`, `space_heat_capacity_joules_per_kelvin`, `space_heat_scale`, `space_temperature_kelvin` | SS14 Temperature exchange and initial state; mass is the local Minecraft fixture's circle-area × density stand-in, and the three `space_*` coefficients are local vacuum modeling only. |
| `TemperatureDamage` | `heat_damage_threshold_kelvin`, `cold_damage_threshold_kelvin`, `heat_damage_per_second`, `cold_damage_per_second`, `damage_cap` | SS14 TemperatureDamage threshold-based heat/cold damage. |
| `ThermalRegulator` | `normal_body_temperature_kelvin`, `metabolism_heat_joules_per_second`, `radiated_heat_joules_per_second`, `implicit_heat_regulation_joules_per_second`, `sweat_heat_regulation_joules_per_second`, `shivering_heat_regulation_joules_per_second`, `thermal_regulation_threshold_kelvin` | SS14 ThermalRegulator body-only heat terms. |

Human and pig retain their previous explicit values, one-second cadence, gas-energy-before-body commit, and strict exterior-vacuum cooling. An evacuated finite room is not a proven exterior vacuum. Unknown/provisional samples cannot exchange heat; an already saved temperature can still take threshold damage when `TemperatureDamage` exists. Without `TemperatureDamage`, no thermal damage runs; without `ThermalRegulator`, no regulation runs. `Temperature` alone supports atmosphere exchange and AdjustTemperature. The persisted BodyTemperature attachment and bridge remain unchanged; `current_kelvin` initializes only an absent attachment and never replaces a saved temperature. Spectators and disabled atmosphere remain inert. This is a local port, not an assertion of upstream mass fixtures or space heat-storage persistence.
