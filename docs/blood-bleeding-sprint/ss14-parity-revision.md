# SS14 parity revision — blood spill, respiration, and thermal regulation

**Current implementation snapshot (latest coordinator run: 190 GameTests):** The revised lossy blood spill, alternating persisted lung phases, thermal regulation including vacuum cooling, toxic inhalation, and optional pressure exposure are implemented locally. Physiology-focused tests passed, but three unrelated electrical tests failed, so the overall GameTest suite is **not green**. Global atmosphere-enabled scheduled physiology remains unverified. The numbered requirements below retain the design contract; explicit "historical" deviations describe the starting state, not today's runtime. This is not complete SS14 parity.

**Toxic-inhale update:** `LungSystem` now applies prototype-configured typed damage once after a successful authoritative inhale, calculated from the actual committed inhaled mixture and capped across all damage types while preserving their proportions. Vacuum, missing/disabled/unknown samples, rejected exchange, and exhale do not apply inhaled toxins. `Oxygenate` adjusts persisted `LUNGS` saturation through `LungSystem.oxygenate`. Character prototypes explicitly configure `metabolizer_types` (human/animal); absent policy supplies no capability. An automated GameTest puts oxygen reagent in the pig bloodstream and invokes the normal metabolism scheduler, confirming that the real oxygen prototype's condition and effect consume oxygen and increase saturation. Breathing itself does not add blood oxygen reagent; globally enabled scheduled atmosphere integration remains unverified. Automated isolated-atmosphere GameTests cover mixed typed inhale/exhale and refusal paths; the cap is exercised by pure math tests because the shipped prototype rates cannot reach their caps in one breath.

## 1. Temporary blood solution and lossy spill attempt

- Retain the solution-backed circulating `BLOODSTREAM` and actual proportional reagent removal. Accumulate removed mixture in a distinct temporary blood solution, preserving constituents and cent-unit quantities, **only while its volume is at or below** the prototype-owned `bleed_puddle_threshold`. A spill is attempted only when volume is **strictly greater than** that threshold; equality does not trigger it. Temporary state may briefly exceed threshold while staging the triggering removal, but must not remain over threshold after the attempt.
- On triggering, make one ordinary `ReagentSystem.handleSpillSolution` attempt at `entity.getOnPos()`, then clear the **entire** temporary mixture whether placement/transfer succeeds, partially succeeds, or fails. Upstream calls `TrySpillAt` and then `RemoveAllSolution()` without conditioning the clear on the spill result. Unplaced material and excess beyond destination capacity are intentionally lost; no later retry or retained failed-spill balance. Account for removal as circulating remainder + material placed + material discarded (including a failed attempt), rather than promising lossless puddle delivery.
- Preserve the Minecraft puddle structural capacity of **1000** units and its existing spread behavior when volume is **greater than 50** (not at equality). This local cap/spread is an adaptation, not upstream puddle capacity parity. Do not add extra spill storage, a multi-puddle delivery guarantee, or capacity backpressure to protect blood from loss. Remove `pending_leak_capacity` from the strict blood prototype contract and remove pending-capacity throttling/backpressure; the temporary attachment is a below-threshold batching store, not a guaranteed-delivery queue. Saved legacy `PENDING_BLOOD_SPILL` material needs an explicit, tested one-time migration/clear rule before changing its format or name; never silently reintroduce an unlimited pending queue.
- Implemented local rule: `BloodReducer.stageBleed` removes the available mixture without a gameplay pending cap; `BloodSystem` triggers only at `> threshold`, makes one ordinary spill attempt, then clears the batch. The bespoke multi-destination `BloodSpillSystem` is retired. Saved valid `PENDING_BLOOD_SPILL` batches at or below threshold remain unchanged and clone/persist; a saved valid overthreshold batch receives one attempt on the next eligible due tick (even at zero bleed rate) and is cleared. Invalid mixture or absent policy remains untouched. No renamed attachment or silent queue migration. The ordinary spill path is server-only and admits at most one puddle's remaining capacity.

## 2. Alternating respiratory updates

- Follow upstream `RespiratorSystem.Update`: at each due **prototype-configured interval**, advance cadence; if dead, do not deplete saturation, switch phase, breathe, or apply suffocation effects. Otherwise deplete saturation on that due update, then, if **not incapacitated**, perform exactly one phase: `INHALE` (environment into lungs) or `EXHALE` (lungs into environment), and switch to the opposite phase for the next update. Incapacitated entities cannot inhale/exhale or advance phase but continue due-update depletion and suffocation damage/recovery evaluation. Assess suffocation against the **post-update** saturation every due update; upstream uses below-threshold damage and at/above-threshold recovery. `IsBreathing` is false while incapacitated and requires saturation strictly above the threshold. Keep any damage/recovery magnitude, depletion amount, thresholds, saturation bounds, and initial phase explicitly prototype-owned or explicitly documented adaptation; never invent a species default or hardcode a per-update rate in Java.
- Require a valid authoritative physical atmosphere sample/exchange for local gas physiology; reject unknown ownership, unloaded/solid cells, provisional reads, disabled atmosphere, and malformed gas/state without mutating phase, gas, or saturation. In a **valid vacuum sample**, there is no oxygen to inhale, but due-update depletion still occurs: empty atmosphere is not the same as an invalid sample. Specify/test how the local fail-closed sample gate differs from upstream's cadence-first update before implementing; do not conflate a missing sample with vacuum or bypass invalid samples to drain saturation.
- **Historical starting deviation (now replaced):** the old lung update exhaled and inhaled in the same tick without a persisted phase or incapacitation branch. The present `LungSystem` instead persists/clone-copies the alternating **next-action phase, not an animation**, preflights a candidate lung update inside `exchangeBreath` **before** any world gas write, and rejects invalid candidates without changing world gas or lung state. An accepted inhale can increase saturation during that update and retains metabolized gas/CO2 for the next exhale. Local gases remain `GasMixture` moles and local `LUNGS` saturation, not upstream lung reagent solutions/metabolism or `RespiratorComponent` saturation; do not claim exact upstream oxygenation.

Implementation note (respirator slice): the persisted phase defaults to `INHALING` for saves lacking it; new and migrated actors therefore begin with a bounded inhale. Human source-derived prototype values are saturation maximum/initial 5, two-second interval, 0.5 L breath, oxygen-to-saturation multiplier 1144, and 2 units depletion per due update. Human asphyxiation damage/recovery is +1/-1 per update; pig is +2/-1. Only proven strict samples reach the due-update reducer; unknown/unloaded/disabled samples neither drain saturation nor apply suffocation. `saturation_loss_per_update` is the prototype-owned due-update depletion; on inhale, actual sampled oxygen increases saturation according to `breath_moles_to_saturation_multiplier` and metabolism retains its remaining gases/produced CO2 until the next exhale. Saturation in `LUNGS` remains authoritative even when an oxygen **reagent** effect increases it: `Oxygenate` and `ModifyLungGas` act on valid existing persisted lungs independently of the world atmosphere enable toggle; scheduled breathing still requires enabled strict atmosphere. Sleeping is the available local incapacitation signal (not a complete SS14 incapacitation/status implementation). Separate world/attachment commits are not crash-atomic. The isolated enabled atmosphere service tests do not establish enabled-global scheduled integration; late lung reconciliation is covered by physiology-focused tests.

Evaluate damage at final body temperature at one-second cadence.
- All gameplay magnitudes and eligibility are character-prototype owned and resolved through existing character identity/`host_entity_types` mapping: metabolic heat, radiative loss, passive/implicit and active regulator strengths, setpoint/ranges, energy/temperature caps and damage parameters. No Java-only `HUMAN` thermal fallback, player/villager type gate, or assumed thermal enrollment for any LivingEntity. Missing `thermal` policy remains inert. Strictly reject unknown/missing nested fields, invalid or non-finite ranges, conflicting host mapping, and inconsistent bounds; preserve saved body state on missing/invalid policy rather than making up defaults.
- Keep atmosphere disabled-by-default and strict physical sampling. Unknown ownership, unloaded/solid cells, provisional samples and invalid state fail closed without treating a provisional ambient read as physical gas. A **proven vacuum-dimension exterior** is a valid authoritative sample of *no atmosphere*: run the body-only space-capacity sink plus regulation, never synthesize breathable/cold gas; finite gas energy exchange remains explicitly committed, whereas body-only cooling is not a finite-cell gas-energy debit. Test physical vacuum separately from absent/invalid sample and finite evacuated rooms. **Historical starting deviation (now replaced):** the former `ThermalExposureMath.expose` path only transferred gas heat and the former `BodyTemperatureSystem` gated to players/villagers. `ThermalProfile.HUMAN` is only a test fixture, not a runtime fallback.

## Prototype contract: inhaled toxins and barotrauma (schema and runtime)

`lungs` requires `toxic_gas_damage_per_mole` (a possibly empty object from exact local `GasType.id()` strings to possibly empty maps of canonical `DamageKeys` to finite nonnegative damage per inhaled mole) and `toxic_gas_damage_cap_per_inhale` (finite nonnegative). Unknown gas IDs, damage keys, wrong types, missing fields, and non-finite/negative values are rejected at their nested paths. Nitrogen is not a toxin by default. Human selects plasma→poison, tritium→radiation and carbon dioxide→poison; pig selects distinct values. This toxic-gas damage map is a local invention, not established upstream parity. The implemented runtime applies proportionally aggregate-capped typed damage once from a successful committed inhale only, not from vacuum, rejected samples/exchange, or exhale.

Independent optional root `pressure` requires, when present, `update_interval_seconds`, `low_hazard_kpa`, `high_hazard_kpa`, `low_damage_per_update`, `high_damage_per_update`, `high_pressure_damage_coefficient`, `max_high_pressure_damage_scale`, `max_matching_damage`, and `ignore_resistances`. Thresholds and interval are positive finite, low < high; coefficient, scale ceiling, accumulated-damage ceiling and typed-map deltas are finite nonnegative. Cadence must round to at least one server tick. Damage maps may be empty and may use different canonical damage types; no species-specific Java enrollment. Missing policy is inert. Implemented runtime uses only strict known atmosphere pressure: a known vacuum has 0 kPa and qualifies as low pressure; unknown, disabled, unloaded or provisional samples are inert, not vacuum. Upstream evidence: `Resources/Prototypes/Body/species_base.yml:173-179` gives human Barotrauma Blunt 0.50 and Heat 0.10; `Entities/Mobs/base.yml:222-225` gives pig Blunt 0.15; `Atmospherics.cs` specifies low 20 kPa with scale 4, high 550 kPa with scale `min((pressure/550 - 1)*4,4)`, one-second cadence and resistance bypass. `BarotraumaComponent` has MaxDamage 200 as a pre-check of current accumulated matching damage: the full hit can cross it, and healing can reopen headroom. This port still uses DamageSystem's 5 typed/health conversion rather than SS14's 100/200 critical/dead thresholds, a separate parity gap. Saturation in local `LUNGS` is the **sole oxygenation authority**: inhaled oxygen or a blood oxygen reagent effect may influence saturation, but neither toxins nor pressure introduce oxygen reagent into `BLOODSTREAM`. SS14's literal blood/organ reagent oxygenation is not reproduced here.

## Subsequent pressure exposure implementation (separate from the blood revision gate)

Optional `CharacterData.pressure()` schedules independently for any mapped living prototype: strict eye-cell `AtmosphereService.sample` only, known vacuum at zero kPa, no inferred pressure from unavailable/disabled/unknown/unloaded/solid cells. Low threshold applies the configured typed delta once; high threshold scales its typed map with a cap on the **scale**, not aggregate damage. A 200-point ceiling is enforced against the current damage ledger's union of the prototype low/high typed keys; unrelated types do not count. Server-side damage uses the configured resistance flag. No pressure-specific attachment or lung/thermal enrollment is created.

Inspected upstream `Content.Server/Atmos/EntitySystems/BarotraumaSystem.cs` (space-wizards/space-station-14, master): upstream has a one-second entity query, warning thresholds, pressure-protection equipment and innate modifiers with weakest exposed slot, immunity event, alerts and admin logs. Upstream pre-checks **current accumulated matching typed damage** against `MaxDamage` 200, skipping at or above it; a hit begun below 200 applies its full scaled damage even if it crosses 200. Healing below 200 reopens headroom: this is not a lifetime ceiling or a per-transaction prorating cap. The local prototype `max_matching_damage` follows that pre-check and separately caps high-pressure *scale*, not total typed damage. Equipment protection, alerts, and other upstream mechanisms remain missing. Upstream also falls back to pressure 1 for missing mixtures; locally an unknown sample is inert by design. These are deliberate scope differences, not perfect numerical SS14 parity.
