# Organ foundation

## Prototypes and datapack migration

Mob character prototypes carry optional `Body`, `InitialBody`, and `Respirator` components. `InitialBody.organs` maps the supported `Lungs` category to a canonical organ prototype ID. Human and pig point to distinct `moonstation14/organ` resources. Organ prototypes are separate catalog data (upstream's organ entity/category distinction), **not** new Minecraft world entities for attached organs. The typed `Lung` organ data owns gas capacity, oxygen uptake multiplier, and toxic-gas tuning; the bounded organ category vocabulary also recognizes `Heart` to allow category-mismatch validation, but no heart attachment or behavior is implemented.

Datapacks must replace the old top-level `lungs` object: move `breath_interval_seconds`, `breath_volume_liters`, `max_saturation`, `initial_saturation`, `min_saturation`, `saturation_loss_per_update`, `suffocation_threshold`, `suffocation_damage_per_update`, `suffocation_recovery_per_update`, and `suffocation_ignore_resistances` into `{ "type": "Respirator", ... }` in character `components`. Move `max_lung_moles`, `breath_moles_to_saturation_multiplier`, `toxic_gas_damage_per_mole`, and `toxic_gas_damage_cap_per_inhale` into the referenced organ prototype's `Lung` object. All fields are required; raw old `lungs` is rejected on concrete and abstract characters, including null. No silent conversion or defaults are applied. Character component inheritance merges `Respirator` by type; it cannot remove a parent component. Body absence remains valid for bodyless characters. Persisted `BODY` organs/respiration are the only supported saved respiratory state.

## Persisted body (phase C1)

`BODY` is the authoritative persisted body snapshot. Its organ list contains stable UUIDs, prototype IDs and categories; a Lungs organ owns only gas moles and temperature (`LungOrganState`). The body separately owns `RespirationState` (saturation, initialized marker and inhale/exhale phase), even when its organ list is empty. Detaching lungs preserves their identity and inventory without resetting mob physiology or regenerating lungs. Explicit attach rejects duplicate identity/category and validates only the incoming prototype; gas updates require an attached lung ID and a current matching prototype, while body respiration updates do not require lungs. Both replace immutable snapshots. Strict codecs reject unknown fields, invalid values, duplicate identities/categories, and noncanonical gas IDs (including case aliases). Persisted saturation must be finite and at least -2; configured `min_saturation` must be at least -2.

Initialization requires a valid bound character with `Body` and `InitialBody` and matching prototypes in the current catalog. `BODY` attachment presence, not organ count or respiratory initialization, marks a saved initialized body. Reads never materialize an attachment default. A newly planned body starts with `RespirationState.UNINITIALIZED` and empty-gas organs; runtime reconciliation initializes saturation from the current policy. An attached saved `BODY`, even one with no organs, remains authoritative and is never regenerated from `InitialBody`. NeoForge persistence and copy-on-death retain the body, its organ gas/temperature and mob respiratory state. There is no saved `LUNG` import or special NBT interception. On encountering an old unknown `LUNG` attachment, NeoForge may log and skip it; no hard old-save error is promised.

Catalog reloads may leave saved organs whose prototypes are missing or have a different category. These are retained as orphans in `BODY`, including their exact lung inventory and UUID: no automatic deletion or recreation. Detach by UUID still works; attaching a valid replacement into a vacated category does not require unrelated saved organs to resolve. A stale Lungs organ is functionally absent for gas exchange and gas effects: with a known due atmosphere sample, saturation depletes without inhaling or exhaling and its payload stays untouched. A stale Heart does not prevent a valid Lungs organ from breathing. Unknown samples do not advance respiration. Noncanonical gas IDs, including mixed-case aliases, are rejected by the `BODY` lung inventory codec.

## Runtime respiration and catalog publication (phase D)

At runtime, saturation loss and suffocation belong to the mob `Respirator`, and can proceed on a known due atmospheric sample even if an attached lung is missing or its prototype has become stale. Gas exchange, gas effects and toxin damage require the actual attached `Lungs` organ to resolve to a current typed `Lung` prototype; swaps and catalog reloads change its tuning without caching the old organ policy. Existing saved organ gas is not silently discarded if the new capacity is lower. A known sample with nonzero gas at 0 K fails closed: it is not treated as vacuum, and no respiration phase, gas or saturation update occurs. A valid empty-gas vacuum sample can still cause due-update depletion. A missing or unknown strict sample is not vacuum and cannot drain saturation or advance phase. As in the prior local implementation, conversion of oxygen moles into saturation and per-inhale toxin damage are local approximations, not claims of exact upstream SS14 gas metabolism or toxicity. SS14's Lung is organ-owned and Respirator is mob-owned; this separation is now reflected in data rather than a character-owned combined lung policy.

The shared server/client PrototypeManager registers organ catalogs alongside characters. For managers registering both types, resolved candidate validation checks every InitialBody reference against the candidate organ catalog before publishing, including staged reloads and authoritative sync; missing or category-mismatched targets reject the entire candidate. Character-only unit-fixture managers cannot resolve references and therefore skip the cross-catalog check. Component fields are audited both on inheritance fragments and on resolved entries; no component tombstone or removal semantics were introduced.
