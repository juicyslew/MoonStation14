## Implemented

- Character `blood` is optional and prototype/identity/host-mapped. Strict policy requires `reference_solution` and `max_volume_modifier`; legacy `initial_volume`, `max_volume`, and the fabricated `bloodloss_damage_cap_per_update` policy fields are rejected. Reference mixture total defines starting fill and modifier-derived cap: human uses `moonstation14:blood` at 300 (cap 600); pig inherits Blood 150 (cap 300), both with modifier 2.0. `BLOOD` persists bleed metadata, while `BLOODSTREAM` owns circulating reagent contents. Old saved scalar volume is ignored and old bleed rate is cleared on initialization.
- Saved pigs containing the formerly configured sulfurblood (in either living store) are **not** converted, refilled, routed, or ticked: reconciliation warns and fails closed, preserving their exact stores. Operators must back up the world and explicitly migrate each such pig's blood attachment under an approved conversion policy; no automatic 3→150 substitution exists. A completely depleted old pig has no sulfur marker and cannot be distinguished from a new depleted pig; its next scheduled refresh can add Blood. Keep the old reagent registered for saved data. This guard also blocks deliberately sulfur-contaminated pigs until reviewed.
- Living-solution migration is fail-closed and idempotent: populated legacy `REAGENT` beside an empty `BLOODSTREAM` migrates exact cents and removes the legacy store when the `BLOOD` initialized marker is absent or false. A true initialized marker preserves both stores fail-closed; intentionally depleted blood is never automatically refilled from the reference mixture. Identical populated stores deduplicate; conflicting populated stores preserve both. Over-cap or invalid contents fail closed; policy and registered constituents are checked before blood enrollment writes. Generic `REAGENT` still serves items, jugs and puddles; `STOMACH` is separate.
- Runtime blood refresh restores reference constituents within reference amounts and modifier-derived capacity. Usable fraction is the minimum reference-constituent fraction; missing constituents count as zero and extras cannot inflate it. Blood constituents are excluded from bloodstream metabolism by policy. Blood reagent effects, typed committed-damage bleed updates, and clone copying are wired; bloodloss damage does not itself increase bleed rate.
- Bleeding removes the actual cent-unit bloodstream mixture into the persisted `PENDING_BLOOD_SPILL` temporary batch without the former gameplay pending cap or destination backpressure. At **strictly greater than** `bleed_puddle_threshold` (not equality), one ordinary spill attempt is made at the entity position and the **entire** batch is cleared, including failed placement or overflow beyond the single destination's remaining 1000-unit capacity. Unplaced blood is intentionally discarded, not retried. Valid saved subthreshold batches persist/clone; valid saved overthreshold batches get one attempt on an eligible due tick, even with zero bleed rate. Invalid batches or missing policy remain untouched.
- On a due tick, a dead actor with an already initialized valid bound blood policy and bloodstream can still drain actual bloodstream contents at its bleed rate, decay that rate, and make the same strictly-overthreshold one-shot spill attempt. Dead actors receive neither refresh nor bloodloss damage/recovery; unbound or uninitialized dead actors do not enroll or create stores. This matches the dead `TickBleed` branch of upstream `SharedBloodstreamSystem.Update` (pinned source linked in `ss14-parity-revision.md`), adapted to local persisted solutions and fail-closed validation.
- Optional prototype-enrolled `LUNGS` stores gas and the **sole local oxygenation authority**, saturation, separately from blood reagents. With a valid enabled strict atmosphere sample, due living updates alternate one inhale or one exhale using a persisted phase (the next action, **not** an animation; legacy saves default to inhale); no same-tick double exchange. Human source-derived policy uses saturation maximum/initial 5, two-second interval, 0.5 L breath, oxygen-to-saturation multiplier 1144 and 2 units depletion per due update; human asphyxiation damage/recovery is +1/-1, pig +2/-1. Inhale can consume oxygen and produce CO2 in the stored mixture, increase saturation, and apply proportionally capped typed toxic-inhale damage from the **committed inhaled mixture**. Valid exhale and sleeping/incapacitated updates deplete saturation; sleep holds the phase and prevents exchange, while dead entities skip. Missing/disabled/unknown samples do not deplete or advance phase; valid vacuum does not supply oxygen. Suffocation uses post-update saturation. `Oxygenate` and `ModifyLungGas` require a valid existing lung and bound policy but **do not require the world atmosphere service to be enabled**. The real oxygen reagent can be metabolized from bloodstream to increase lung saturation; breathing does not add oxygen reagent to blood.
- Prototype-bound thermal updates run at one-second cadence with strict physical samples: finite gas-mediated exchange first, then a **body-only vacuum sink for proven exterior empty vacuum** (never a fake gas inventory or gas-energy write), bounded implicit regulation, metabolic heat minus radiative loss, and eligible active correction. SS14 uses a gameplay-only fake space heat capacity 7000 scaled by atmosphere heat scale 8; the local sink applies its equivalent body heat transfer without fabricating matter. A finite evacuated room does not use this sink. Human policy uses upstream 310.15 K, fixture-derived mass π·0.35²·185 kg, specific heat 42, regulator 800/100/500/2000/2000 J/s; pig has its own fixture-derived π·0.35²·250 kg and regulator 800/100/250/500/500 J/s. Invalid samples do not run regulation; stored-temperature damage may still apply. Active sweating/shivering currently assume both capabilities; no SS14 action-blocker integration is claimed. The source-derived 7000×8 J/K space coefficient and 0.1 transfer efficiency yield approximately **28 K net first-tick human cooling** after regulation.
- Optional prototype `Barotrauma` component schedules pressure damage independently on strict known atmosphere samples; known vacuum qualifies for low-pressure typed damage. Human and pig effective Barotrauma maps and 20/550 kPa thresholds match the cited upstream species and Atmospherics sources in `ss14-parity-revision.md`. High-pressure **scale** is capped at 4; the prototype's 200 ceiling is a **read-only pre-check** of current matching typed damage, using a temporary blunt baseline from missing vanilla health if no ledger exists. A rejected hit creates no damage ledger; an eligible hit imports that baseline once at commit and applies the full hit, which can cross 200. The next hit stops until healing reopens headroom. Missing/disabled/unknown samples are inert. Direct component codec decode and encode enforce the strict schema, even outside whole-character parsing. No equipment protection or alerts are implemented; local DamageSystem still converts 5 typed damage per health.
- Normal prototype reload candidate validation checks blood reagent references before publication; catalog publication invalidates blood policy caches. Separately, scheduled lung exchange preflights the next lung candidate before committing a world gas write, so rejected candidates leave world gas and lung state untouched. Direct `PrototypeManager` publication can bypass normal listener validation and does not prove a live datapack reload.

- Coverage includes blood migration/empty-stream authority and lossy threshold spill cases, alternating lung phases and toxic-inhale paths, thermal regulation/vacuum, pressure low/high hazards and missing-ledger prechecks, strict direct component codec behavior, and oxygen-reagent metabolism. Unit/codec tests cover relevant policies, arithmetic, state and candidate-validation paths. The isolated atmosphere tests use an enabled private `AtmosphereService`, **not** the globally enabled scheduled entity-physiology path. Next verification: investigate the unrelated villager travel-ownership failure before declaring suite acceptance.
- Earlier intermittent movement, atmosphere and fire GameTest failures are not characterized as blood/lung regressions or generally resolved; movement remains flaky. Owner's manual pig attack check yielded generic attack/blunt behavior only, not a pig-specific multiplier. No manual gameplay acceptance, world save/restart, crash-interruption recovery, or live loaded-datapack reload is established.
- Entity/world writes (lung gas/room gas, body heat/room energy, blood batch/puddle) are **not crash-atomic**. Intentional blood discard after a failed or partial spill is separate from possible crash interruption; isolated in-process tests do not prove crash-safe conservation.

## Remaining scope

- Global atmosphere remains disabled by default. No test establishes globally enabled **scheduled** lung, thermal, or pressure integration. Unresolved local adaptations include the invented toxic-gas damage map, local `max_lung_moles` limits, and the 5 typed damage per Minecraft health bridge instead of SS14's critical/dead damage thresholds of 100/200. Local gas/saturation is not SS14 blood/organ oxygenation or organ metabolism timing; thermal capability blockers and pressure equipment protection are missing. Full physiology or perfect numerical parity is not claimed.
- Injection/transfusion, blood typing, DNA/forensics, fire exposure, medical alerts/examination, organ interactions, full SS14 multi-solution internals, connected/manual gameplay and 20-player performance remain outside verified acceptance. Full parity and acceptance of the earlier extension contracts are **not** claimed.
