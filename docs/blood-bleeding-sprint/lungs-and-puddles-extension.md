# Lungs and puddles — additive implementation and acceptance status

> **Historical implementation record:** the approved [`ss14-parity-revision.md`](ss14-parity-revision.md) supersedes this document where spill retention/threshold or respiratory phase semantics conflict. Tests/status below describe the earlier implementation, not acceptance of the revision.

This additive update supersedes the historical lung and blood-spill deferrals in [`Instructions.md`](Instructions.md), [`architecture.md`](architecture.md), and [`solution-extension.md`](solution-extension.md); those historical records are intentionally not rewritten. It documents implemented behavior and remaining acceptance, not full SS14 parity. Atmosphere integration defaults disabled.

## Implemented: prototype policy and persisted lung state

- `lungs` is an optional strict character-prototype record, independent of `blood`, `thermal`, and `movement`. There is no Java species eligibility rule. Human and pig have distinct explicit lung policies; omitting lung policy does not enroll a character or create respiratory defaults.
- When present, all fields are required: `breath_interval_seconds`, `breath_moles_per_update`, `max_lung_moles`, `oxygen_consumption_moles_per_update`, `carbon_dioxide_production_moles_per_update`, `max_saturation`, `initial_saturation`, `saturation_gain_per_update`, `saturation_loss_per_update`, `suffocation_threshold`, `suffocation_damage_per_update`, `suffocation_recovery_per_update`, and `suffocation_ignore_resistances`. Unknown fields and nulls are rejected. Numeric values are finite and bounded; intervals resolve to positive 20 Hz server ticks. Saturation is a prototype-defined unit in `[0, max_saturation]`, not inherently a percentage. Metabolic rates are per lung update, not atmospheric concentration ratios.
- Lung state is persisted separately from both bloodstream and generic reagents: it owns canonical atmosphere gas IDs and quantities, temperature, saturation, and an initialization marker. Codecs reject malformed gas/state data. A missing lung attachment initializes from vacuum gas and configured initial saturation during reconciliation; invalid or uninitialized runtime state fails closed rather than inventing a valid breath.

## Implemented: exchange, metabolism, and effects

- Prototype cadence drives scheduled inhalation/exhalation through strict `AtmosphereService.exchangeBreath`. Requests are bounded by configured demand and lung capacity. Actual former gas is exchanged with the room; oxygen consumption is bounded by available lung oxygen, carbon-dioxide production follows actual consumption, and saturation gain/loss is bounded by configured policy. Suffocation damage applies below threshold and recovery above it.
- Atmosphere sampling/exchange rejects disabled atmosphere, unknown ownership, unloaded/solid cells, and invalid requests. A proven exterior supplies ambient gas without finite-cell debit/credit; finite claimed-cell exchange writes the calculated mixture atomically through the service API. No provisional UI sample is used for physiology.
- `Oxygenate` adjusts existing valid saturation only; `ModifyLungGas` applies signed deltas to the lung inventory, not the room. Both require enabled global atmosphere plus valid persisted state and supported character policy. Gas ratio keys are canonical atmosphere gas names, never reagent identifiers.
- Important integration limit: `LungExchangeGameTests` enable a **private isolated** `AtmosphereService.withVacuumDimensions(...)` and verify the service exchange contract (finite composition/energy conservation, failure without mutation, exterior behavior, and unknown/unloaded/solid rejection). They do not enable the global service and do not execute an entity's scheduled lung tick with global atmosphere enabled. Source-level implementation and reducer/unit coverage are not a substitute for that integration acceptance.

## Implemented: pending blood spill and puddle routing

- Each blood policy strictly configures positive `bleed_puddle_threshold` and `pending_leak_capacity` as finite cent-representable solution volumes, with capacity at least threshold. Human and pig configurations are explicit and distinct. The threshold is a batching trigger, not permission to lose mixture; pending capacity is a hard bound.
- Bleeding stages proportional removal of actual mixed bloodstream contents in cent units into a persisted pending-spill solution. Reaching threshold attempts routing into supported nearby existing puddles and, where possible, newly placed puddles. Partial destination capacity is used and the exact remaining constituent amounts stay pending. Multiple puddles can receive an oversized batch. Each Minecraft puddle's structural reagent capacity is fixed at 1000, a deliberate adaptation rather than upstream-configurable capacity.
- Full/invalid destinations, unavailable placement/support, and pending-capacity pressure fail closed: no intentional discard, and blood removal is bounded by available pending capacity. Routing tests cover exact composition, existing/new destinations, partial/multiple-puddle delivery, full or blocked destinations, and backpressure.
- The exactness claim is for in-process staged routing and automated cases, not crash/restart transactions. Puddle/world reagent updates and entity pending-spill updates live in separate persistence stores; they are **not crash-atomic**. Similarly, finite atmosphere world writes and entity lung-state writes are not one crash-atomic transaction. No absolute losslessness across crash/save interruption is claimed.

## Verification and remaining acceptance

- Latest coordinator-reported build passed and the isolated GameTest run passed **176 required automated GameTests**. Earlier attempts had intermittent movement, atmosphere, and fire GameTest failures; do not attribute them to lungs/blood or claim those flakes are generally resolved. This documentation-only closeout did not rerun build or GameTests.
- Atmosphere remains disabled by default. Enabling and validating the global service with actual scheduled entity lung ticks, persisted lung updates, and effects remains open. No live world save/restart, crash/recovery test, or loaded datapack reload has been performed.
- Major remaining parity/acceptance gaps include injection/transfusion, DNA/forensics, blood typing, toxin/pressure/fire exposure, medical alerts/examination, organ interaction, respiratory blood oxygenation, and full SS14 multi-solution internals. Networked/manual gameplay and 20-player acceptance/performance remain untested. This extension makes no claim of complete SS14 parity.
