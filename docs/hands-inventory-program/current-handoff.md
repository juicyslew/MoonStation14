## Policy and current implementation

- Only a controlled `CHARACTER` body with prototype-declared hand capability is eligible: human has left/right hands; pig has none. Hands and wearable slots belong to the body; grid/container contents belong to a storage item, not an intrinsic body inventory. Legacy `HANDS` tokens are metadata, not live `ItemStack` ownership.
- Persistent `LIVE_HANDS` holds ordered hand slots, active hand, occupant token and real `ItemStack` with bounded codec and revision checks. Lifecycle and experimental ready paths bootstrap the attachment for eligible bodies. `HandActorAuthority` requires an exact controlled body/epoch, account-owned `CREATIVE_PARKED_INVENTORY` marker and clean isolated carrier, and revalidates authority for actions.
- Server-side whole-stack `ItemEntity` pickup and drop transfer ownership between world and body hands, with a server ray/reach/occlusion check for pickup, revision/token checks and compensation on known failure paths. The follow-up shares a small aiming tolerance for targeting and uses short safe forward drop placement with fallback. Ambiguous outcomes return `RECOVERY_REQUIRED`, potentially requiring admin intervention; they are not proof of automatic repair or crash-safe atomicity.
- Authenticated server packet query/action routes provide hand state and gated pickup/drop intents; client G pickup / H drop and V active-hand toggle controls with an active-hand HUD. V requests server-authenticated selection of the other hand on an eligible two-hand body, with revision checks; it does not grant client authority over hand state. The follow-up makes the HUD/chat layout safer, restores a crosshair and shows decimal health and `DAMAGE`. The client only nominates a target; the server decides. Sprint 10c adds the limited pouch below; 10d adds a client-only bounded I screen, not a vanilla container menu.
- `CreativeParkedInventory` persists account-bound exact snapshot data in the **same vanilla playerdata attachment**. The runtime parks/physically clears the actual Creative carrier before eligible body control and restores only in Creative across routine lifecycle and experimental mode transitions. Empty Creative carriers can receive an empty isolation marker. Packet and selected direct-API guards are bounded; raw inventory-list mutation and other direct paths are **not** covered by a complete barrier. Unsupported/occupied extra compartments are rejected rather than silently quarantined. The old external `CreativeQuarantineStore` journal, reconciliation classifier and playerdata disk probes remain inert/deferred, not the active park/restore path; no independent durable index or sudden power-loss guarantee is claimed.
- Sprint 10c registers a nonstackable pouch item with **one item-owned whole-stack cell**. A controlled eligible body can move a supported whole stack between the pouch and its other hand via J insert / K extract; the body does not own the pouch cell. Server authority checks the controlled body/epoch/lease and hand tokens/revision, and the client HUD displays a bounded pouch hint/content summary rather than opening a menu. Nested pouches and explicitly excluded stack-bearing components are rejected. See [sprint 10c pouch](sprint-10c-pouch.md) for the exact boundary.
- Sprint 10d declares ordered belt/back `EquipmentSlots` on the human prototype, with no implicit equipment slots for pig or undeclared bodies. Equipment occupants live beside hands in the **same body-owned `LIVE_HANDS` attachment**; hand↔slot equip/unequip and hand↔equipped storage cell store/take publish a single revisioned attachment transition after server authority, layout, slot/item, occupancy and token checks. Old `LIVE_HANDS` saves with absent optional equipment remain compatible; the declared empty layout materializes on first equip. Unknown or occupied slots fail closed. Registered nonstackable belt/back `BELT`/`BAG` items each reuse the item-owned, one-whole-stack `PouchContents` cell (as does the held pouch); belt accepts only belt and back only bag. The client-only I screen displays two hands, held pouch and its cell, belt/back and their cells, with active-hand selection and Equip/Unequip/Store/Take buttons. It exposes no vanilla player container menu, armor or hotbar transport. Death/corpse equipment remains body-owned; no automatic drop is added. See [sprint 10d equipment and UI](sprint-10d-equipment-ui.md).
