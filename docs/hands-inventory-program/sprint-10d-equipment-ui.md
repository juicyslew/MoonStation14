**Status: current one-cell playable slice closed by owner decision; connected subset accepted.** Owner reports no kicks, equipped bag and belt, stored objects in both, preserved the same state across logout/login, dropped and recovered the bag with its contained item intact, and removed the belt item before dropping belt and item. They could not store a pouch in a bag or belt; nesting is deliberately denied by this mod. This does not claim held-pouch J/K, every I-screen button or Creative-tab path, or two-client acceptance. The screen's exhaustive acceptance and untested paths remain caveats. This is not a full SS14 inventory or general Minecraft container menu.

## Implemented boundary

- The human prototype declares ordered `EquipmentSlots` `belt` and `back`; pig does not acquire implicit equipment or hands. Undeclared/unavailable prototypes are not assumed to have either slot. The slot capability belongs to the body; a belt or bag's contents belong to that item's `ItemStack`.
- Equipment occupants (token and real stack) are stored next to the hands in the **existing body-owned `LIVE_HANDS` attachment**, not in a second inventory, vanilla armor or hotbar. Server-authorized hand↔slot Equip/Unequip and hand↔equipped-item cell Store/Take validate the bound body's declared layout, expected revision/tokens, item type and source/destination occupancy before publishing one new attachment revision. Belt accepts the registered nonstackable `BELT`; back accepts the registered nonstackable `BAG`. A filled bag or belt can be unequipped into an empty hand without emptying its cell. Rejections do not authorize a client-side move; ambiguous publication can yield `RECOVERY_REQUIRED`.
- `LIVE_HANDS` codec equipment is optional so old saves without it remain compatible with a matching declared prototype; first successful equip materializes the empty declared layout. A present but mismatched layout or unknown slot is not silently reinterpreted, and occupied destinations fail closed. On death the equipment remains attached to the body/corpse; there is **no automatic drop** or new corpse loot flow.
- Each registered pouch, bag and belt uses the same nonstackable, **one whole-stack cell** `PouchContents` component. Held pouch J/K transfers remain available; a worn belt/back has separate Store/Take actions from/to the designated hand. The child's token/stack stays with its containing item across hand/equipment movement. Nested storage items and explicit native stack-bearing components are excluded; the explicit list does not prove that arbitrary custom components cannot carry stacks. Child count is bounded to 1–64 and the item's own stack maximum, but arbitrary component byte size is not globally bounded.
- I opens a client-only `BodyInventoryScreen` showing two hand summaries and active-hand selection, held pouch and its cell, equipped belt/back and each item's cell. Buttons send server requests for Equip, Unequip, Store and Take; the screen is a snapshot presentation, not draggable real slots, a vanilla player container menu, armor or hotbar access. It does not add a multi-cell grid or garment pockets.
