# Plant sampling composition (unit 1)

## Pinned source and packet contract

Audited SS14 checkout `c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91`. The packet prototype is `SeedBase` (`Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Objects/Specific/Hydroponics/seeds.yml`); its parent chain resolves through `BaseItem` and `BasePaperLabelable` in `Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Objects/Misc/paper.yml`. The resolved packet composition includes `SeedComponent` defaults (`HealthOverride: null`) and the inherited item/paper-label components. `PlantHolderSystem` consumes the packet's seed data when planting: its initial health is the override when present, otherwise seed endurance; planting starts at age 1. This health is packet state, not a gene or profile field.

Source references: `Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Objects/Specific/Hydroponics/seeds.yml` (`SeedBase` and children); `Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Objects/Misc/paper.yml` (`BasePaperLabelable`); `Resources/Prototypes/Entities/Objects/Base/base_items.yml` (`BaseItem`); `Content.Shared/Hydroponics/Components/SeedComponent.cs`; `Content.Server/Hydroponics/Systems/PlantHolderSystem.cs` planting and seed-packet creation paths. The implementation reuses immutable `GeneticValue`/`PlantSeedReference` for seed identity and stores optional health separately in `SEED_PACKET_HEALTH_OVERRIDE` (`SeedPacketHealthOverride`). `GeneticCarriers.createSeedPacket` is the common registered-packet constructor; extraction calls it with no override. Planting passes the resolved optional override to `HydroponicsTrayBlockEntity.commitPlant` / `PlantInstance.plant(reference, now, override)`. Absent override preserves resolved endurance; a present raw value, including negative, is not clamped at packet creation or planting. The ordinary plant health check subsequently kills unhealthy plants.

## Sampling mapping and boundary

The upstream sample-taker tool is tagged `Wirecutter`, `Haycutters`, or `Clippers`; `CUTTING` quality is not the source selector. Source interaction dispatch is hoe, shovel, sample, then sharp-tool behavior. Sampling costs `30` health when ready and `40` otherwise (`(3,5) * 10`), and the health override represents post-loss health. Ready samples store null override; non-ready samples store post-loss health. Do not clamp it when creating the packet; holder sanity handling and later health polling remain separate source behaviors.

Successful sampling marks the shared seed reference only after packet publication, emits scream after publication, then performs the 30% sampled-flag roll. Poll-end sampling has distinct behavior and does not scream. `Sampled` is false by default and is reset by `RemovePlant`; harvesting does not reset it. A sampled packet retains the same seed reference. Local wirecutter/tag/profile material already exists and should be reused by the follow-on interaction unit.

The implementation now dispatches the existing registered wirecutter through ordinary authenticated `BodyHandActionNetworking` → `BotanyHandUseService` USE. Hoe/shovel precedence remains earlier; sampling is selected from resolved `ToolProfile.botanySampleTaker`, not cutting quality or item ID. Eligibility uses the shared source-equivalent uncapped raw growth stage `max(1, (int)(age * growthStages / maturation))`, not an age>1 proxy or clamped visual stage. It stages one packet through `FreshWorldOutputBatch.publishBodyHarvest`'s body-output callback path, commits raw post-loss holder health and marks that exact reference shared only after publication, attempts trusted pickup, sends best-effort Minecraft chat feedback, plays the shared prepared plant scream, rolls the 30% sampled flag, then unconditionally clamps holder health to `[0,endurance]` without immediately killing it. Output/pickup recovery is surfaced as `RECOVERY_REQUIRED` without rolling back committed state; an ordinary pickup denial leaves the packet in the world. Tool ownership/count/durability are unchanged. Post-commit completion is in `finally`, so an audio/feedback failure cannot bypass the sampled roll and clamp.
