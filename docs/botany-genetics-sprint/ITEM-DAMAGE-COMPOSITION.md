# Item damage composition (implementation in progress)

This step adds immutable stack-owned `ItemDamageState` (canonical accumulated damage plus impact deadline), the pure `ItemDamageKernel`, and optional typed `item_damage` composition on resolved `LooseObjectProfile`. Tomato's item registration selects its inherited `moonstation14:tomato` profile. Its supported biological groups intentionally omit metaphysical/holy; only authored impact/throw-land self damage, destruction threshold/sound, and current-reagent spill policy are represented.

The kernel reuses `DamageKeys.validateState` / `DamageReducer.applyDelta` / `total`, compares source-speed values in float precision with the source's strict threshold, and converts horizontal blocks/tick to source horizontal units/second at 20 ticks/second. `LooseItemEntity` now consumes resolved compositions on actual horizontal block contact using its own pre-contact XZ velocity; vertical gravity-only contact cannot trigger impact damage. `BodyLooseThrow` marks landing eligibility only after publication, and the first downward floor contact consumes its persisted entity marker once. This first-floor adapter is intentionally not represented as timed SS14 `LandEvent` parity.

Threshold damage commits the stack ledger before guarded profile-driven feedback, loaded-only current-reagent spill attempt, and item discard. Jug and item destruction reuse the same loaded floor/puddle search and `ReagentSystem.handleSpillSolution`; jug remainder policy is unchanged. A destroyed item is discarded after its spill attempt even if no loaded surface or full admission is available. Generic/jug profiles remain without item damage. Source impact-hit sound is unresolved and omitted; entity hard-fixture contact, target damage, stun, and red-flash behavior remain unsupported/unverified per the design/source audit. Audio rights remain unknown; playback routes through the existing controlled-camera sound dispatch.

These native cases are authored evidence only, not a native runtime pass. They do not establish restart persistence or client audio audibility. Unsupported boundaries remain as stated above, and no full readiness or owner acceptance is claimed.
