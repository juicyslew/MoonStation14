# Care showcase implementation slice

The planned care wing is a second bounded `11×12×5` footprint at `mainOrigin + (13,0,0)`, with 11 ordinary-trigger station descriptors K–U. Each descriptor associates an adjacent tray/sign and names reagent, hundredth-unit dose, action, and an observation rather than claiming a guaranteed outcome. The descriptor set covers health, toxins, pests, weeds, potency, RobustHarvest, Cryoxadone, growth, Diethylamine gene rolls, Phalanximine, and seed restoration.

Dose/effect notes follow `M5-CARE-COMPOSITION.md` and the existing typed reagent definitions. Thresholds are evaluated at poll entry; one poll drains one unit per reagent. Soil polls occur every 60 ticks and drain 1u; Phalanximine uses 400 cents to meet the exact threshold. The 300-cycle baseline may drift; do not promise precise delayed values. Stochastic growth/genetic/seed restoration and RobustHarvest yield changes are explicitly not promised. Prepare baselines through fixture scenarios; soil is to remain empty until player action. A true seed-restoration transition also gives the localized actionbar notice described in [RESTORATION-FEEDBACK.md](RESTORATION-FEEDBACK.md); it is neither anchored world text nor guaranteed by a finite dose.

Admin setup prepares resolved-wheat plant baselines in all eleven care trays, leaves their soil solutions empty, and grants one labelled registered beaker per station. Use the ordinary beaker-on-tray interaction; do not expect automatic dosing. Bottles K–Q contain 1u (one use); R/S/U contain 10u and require two selected 5u transfers; T contains 4u (one use). A qualifying ordinary poll processes present reagent at poll start and drains one unit, so Phalanximine crosses its 4u threshold then drains only 1u on that poll. The 1u/4u bottles are sized for one selected transfer; the 10u bottles need two.

K health baseline has headroom for ammonia's health/nutrition change. L toxins starts at 39 and M pests at 6, with the counterpart stat at zero. N weeds starts at 6. Q is mature-but-not-ready with health/toxin headroom; Cryoxadone rewinds age and forces one evaluator cycle, but do not promise a visible stage change. R is immature; growth is probabilistic and only observable if growth crosses a stage threshold. Look for a stage change; clock-based age advancement alone is not proof, and no timing is guaranteed. S Diethylamine has two independent 10% lifespan/endurance rolls; neither change is observable through ordinary packet examination (which reports yield/potency only). This station is unsupported for manual verification and is not a completed observable showcase. T starts alive and nonviable; qualifying Phalanximine restores viability. U starts seedless, ready, and repeat-harvest with health headroom; each qualifying Sedin poll independently has a 20% seed-restoration chance. No restoration popup or seed display exists. A crop harvested before Sedin's restoration is not later updated when its tray changes. The finite 10u supply covers two 5u transfers (and qualifying polls drain reagent); there is no promised free indefinite retry or extra beaker. If no poll restores seeds or no attempt succeeds, acknowledge the stochastic trial may fail and requires another prepared trial without automatic dosing. Do not infer mutation from a preset.
