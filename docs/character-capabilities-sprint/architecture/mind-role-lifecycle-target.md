# Mind, role and lifecycle — target architecture (decision, not implementation)

**Status: owner target decision only.** This describes desired future gameplay and authorization boundaries, not a feature enabled by this sprint or an accepted connected test. It supersedes older plan-only assumptions that the initial join must immediately create a playable body or that role selection is an implicit corpse possession. It does not supersede the [current handoff](../current-handoff.md) for what is implemented or tested. Any migration from the existing model needs separately scoped, owner-approved stages; do not enable the default-off lifecycle gate or change ownership, code, assets, builds or configuration on the strength of this document.

## Entry and role selection

- Initial game join enters an **explicit server-owned lobby state before map visibility or control**. The lobby is not a playable world body and grants no world actions, map view or implicit ghost access. Appearance, role and character details are requested through a UI overlay/menu and validated and committed by the server. The server decides eligibility, availability and allocation; client selections are intents, never grants.
- The presentation/physical representation of the lobby is undecided. A far-away ghost is a suggestion **not adopted** as the lobby design. The prior no-harness exception applies **only** to an explicit lobby state, never to ordinary active character or ghost play. Entering/leaving the lobby must not silently manufacture a body, grant control or bypass an unresolved durable claim.
- On a successful selection, the selected character and role become the Mind's **sole currently controlled body**. Ordinary living play cannot switch into ghost or possess a different body; death and explicitly gated development tools are the only exceptions. A role is not permission to operate a second body.

## Death, ghost and return

| Transition | Target behavior |
|---|---|
| Current role body dies | Retain the body/corpse in the world; transition the same Mind to a ghost tethered to **that current role body**, with a same-body revival claim. Death does not grant a new living role automatically. |
| Ghost activity | Ghost may see the map and other ghosts, roam, view-follow entities without owning or controlling them, haunt under separately defined server permissions, and teleport to its **own current tethered corpse**. Following a view is not a Mind transfer. These actions are future features, not current behavior. |
| Own corpse revived | Request the Mind's consent before returning it to the revived body. Revival must not force a return. Until consent is resolved, do not create simultaneous control or silently redirect the Mind. Prompt timing, timeout and disposition of a revived body after declined consent remain open. |
| Ghost chooses a different role | A role-selection UI offers specific server-authorized options (for example stragglers, pets or hostile roles), not arbitrary right-click possession. Server validates eligibility and role availability again at commit. Successful transfer **irreversibly forfeits** the previous body's active and revival claim, unless an explicit new authorized grant later creates a new claim. The prior corpse may still exist and be revivable in the world; its former Mind has no automatic restoration right. The new role body is the sole current tether; if it dies, its corpse is the new ghost tether and may support consent-based revival. |

Normal right-click body possession is **not** the player role-return flow. Keep any existing operator/debug right-click possession explicitly gated and separate from production role selection; do not bridge experimental possession into lifecycle authority. View-follow/spectating is view-only even when looking through another entity's camera; it must never turn into possession or authorization through proximity, click or camera targeting.

## Authority and persistence contract for later design

- One stable Mind, one current owned harness/role claim and one effective ownership epoch at a time outside the explicit lobby exception. The ghost and the corresponding current-role corpse/revival claim are related lifecycle states, **not two simultaneously controlled bodies**. Earlier roles can remain as reporting/audit history, never as live ownership, action authority or automatic revival eligibility.
- Make selection and handoff atomic and durable across profile, body and session authority. Invalidate the previous epoch, actions, menus and pending claims on transfer; reject stale/replayed actions and competing owners. Reconnect, disconnect and crash/save disagreement must fail closed on ambiguity: no duplicate body, dual owner, guessed winner, automatic claim restoration or fallback ghost/lobby that erases evidence. Do not promise existing v1 persistence provides cross-save atomicity; design recovery and migration explicitly.
- Role/body eligibility and action capability are declared by validated prototypes, with server-owned availability and permissions layered on top. Species is not an implicit action grant: a pig without hands cannot use hand-only actions. An available body is not automatically possessable, and an operator configure marker is not a production role grant. Server resolves exact session, current epoch, selected role/body, eligibility, permissions and action capability at commit time. Bound search and work for eventual 20-player use, including competing selections and revival races; that scale is a goal, not verified evidence.
- SS14 is an inspiration for separating Mind `TransferTo` from ghost view-follow. This is a proposed Minecraft-specific role UI and lobby contract; do **not** assert SS14 has this exact menu, lobby, consent prompt or role policy without source evidence.

## Current implementation versus target

The present lifecycle schema v1 persists an original body/corpse identity and its death claim; it does **not** represent an arbitrarily changing current-role tether, role history with forfeiture, or consent-based revival. Current lifecycle ghost sessions and the separate experimental `/ms14 mindghost possess` path do not compose into a production ghost-to-role transfer. Existing bounded spectator-camera suppression is not view-follow. There is no implemented server-owned pre-map lobby, appearance/role/details UI flow, haunt, own-corpse teleport, durable cross-species role transfer, or revival consent. No code path, gate, runtime capability or test acceptance is established by this decision record; see [current handoff](../current-handoff.md) for observed partial ghost-entry and unresolved connected-control evidence.

The current display-only `Offline` corpse badge is based on exact bound-account server presence, not durable current-role ownership. A connected character, Creative carrier or ghost should not show `Offline` on its bound body; a separate transient 60-second grace starts on observed disconnection and may restart visually after chunk reload or restart. The newer owner parked-body death -> ghost test exposed the wrong `Offline` badge while still connected; a narrow display fix passed pure connected-policy GameTests, but its connected retest is pending. Once a ghost takes another role, the former corpse requires a distinct explicit state/label rather than `Offline`, with the badge referring to the **current tether**, not permanently to the originally bound account. Do not implement or choose final wording until durable current-role claim and forfeiture are designed and owner-reviewed; this visual fix changes no schema or authority.

## Narrow decisions still required

1. Lobby presentation method, without adopting the far-away ghost suggestion by default.
2. Revival prompt timing/timeouts and disposition of the revived body when consent is declined or absent, without forced Mind return.
3. Role allocation/source: spawn versus takeover of consenting or AI-bound bodies, including prototype eligibility, availability, permissions and round policy.
4. Old corpse disposition after forfeiture, independently of the former owner's lost claim.
5. Owner-reviewed former-corpse badge state and exact wording after a durable current-role claim/forfeiture design; do not conflate forfeiture with disconnection.

## Migration and Test-Path (future, owner-gated)

First obtain owner approval for small, explicit stages: lobby and admission policy; server-validated role catalog/UI; durable current-role/claim schema migration and atomic transfer/recovery rules; death/revival consent and ghost abilities; then multiplayer acceptance. Specify compatibility with existing v1 saves and experimental sessions before changing authority or enabling any gate. Each stage needs fail-closed unit and dedicated-server tests for state/epoch, forfeiture, reconnect/crash ambiguity, competing selections and action rejection; follow with **owner-only** connected disposable-world checks for initial no-map lobby, selected-body entry, death/tether/view-follow versus control, consent, new-role forfeiture and repeat death/revival. Include two-client contention and bounded 20-player work. Automated tests cannot stand in for connected acceptance. This document runs no tests and authorizes no implementation stage.
