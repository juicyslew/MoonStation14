## Narrow candidate, not vanilla parity

An explicitly enumerated **mod-owned block, no-item interaction** may be investigated after an audited body-actor and permission adapter exists. It must bind the exact session/body/epoch and capability, recheck body status, loaded target, face, server reach/visibility, protection and cancellation at commit, and call only an audited body-compatible handler whose side effects and event contract are documented. If the handler or protection route still requires a `ServerPlayer`, stop and ask the owner; do not silently call the carrier. This candidate is an adapted subset only, **not** proof of generic vanilla block activation or full block-use acceptance.

**Generic empty-hand block breaking is BLOCKED** until exact-version research proves how a controlled body preserves mining duration/progress and interruption, tool/harvest rules, permission/protection, event ordering/cancellation and correct drops under contention. `Level.destroyBlock` as an instant break and a fake player as authority are not acceptable substitutes. An adapted, explicitly enumerated subset is possible only after evidence and owner review; do not label it vanilla break parity.

## Decision and follow-up

M0 source audit and M1 authoritative admission may proceed independently of live item ownership. M2 may investigate a conditional, narrow no-item mod-block subset only after its adapter is audited; generic empty-hand mining and generic vanilla block use remain at the stop gate. Record per-route source evidence, negative/positive tests and an owner decision before implementation/acceptance claims. Keep the owner's requested basic actions, including block mine/use, in the final acceptance inventory; any permanent exclusion or scope narrowing requires explicit owner consent. Item-dependent paths retain their existing ownership/isolation gates. This note neither enables the lifecycle path nor changes any other sprint's ownership.
