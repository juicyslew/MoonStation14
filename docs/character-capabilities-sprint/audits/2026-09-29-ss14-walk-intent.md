# 2026-09-29 SS14 walk intent — bounded decision and evidence

- **Owner decision:** Pinned SS14 upstream `InputMoverComponent.cs` at commit `c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91` has `Sprinting => (HeldMoveButtons & MoveButtons.Walk)==0`. Default movement is fast; holding Shift selects walk for grounded controlled CHARACTER. Stay as close to SS14 as feasible without changing prototypes. Crouch animation may remain, but crouch mechanics are not a design target. Prone is a distinct future prototype/body-state action for a qualifying race; passage through a 1x1 tunnel depends on race size or prone, not generic vanilla crouch.
- **Implementation boundary:** The `GhostControlClient.intentButtons` change selects only the CHARACTER speed bit (`BUTTON_SPRINT` on without Shift, off with Shift), independent of Minecraft sprint state; jump is preserved. Prediction and server movement read the same intent bit. Existing human prototype speeds remain 2.5 walk / 4.5 fast, pig 4 / 4; these are not new species-wide acceleration or speed assumptions. GHOST retains Shift-descend and its existing sprint mapping; vanilla Creative and free Spectator are unaffected. The legacy direct-player experimental route has not been migrated. Do not claim universal SS14 movement parity.
- **Evidence and open risks:** Focused intent-policy tests cover CHARACTER default fast, Shift walk even with Minecraft sprint, jump, and unchanged GHOST mapping. `./gradlew.bat test compileGametestJava` passed as reported; there is no owner connected retest of this change. Airborne and collision speed, AI behavior, and SS14 policy/status speed multipliers need measurement/audit before further fidelity claims. M1 authoritative action admission and a mod-owned no-item pilot are next; movement measurement and upstream audit can run in parallel, followed by the separately gated role-transfer work.
