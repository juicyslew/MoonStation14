The naming/owner rule is proposed, not historical. Entity-only `ComplexInteractionAttachment` and `ComplexInteractionSystem` are now implemented; neither creates an item data component nor a world action.

## Owner decision and implemented boundary

- Keep the vanilla `Spectator` carrier and camera `FOLLOW` of exactly the currently owned ghost or character. In exact Minecraft 1.21.1 source, `ServerGamePacketListenerImpl.handleTeleportToEntityPacket` processes spectator entity selection by teleporting the carrier; `ServerPlayer.teleportTo` calls `setCamera(this)`. Separately, Spectator `ServerPlayer.attack` calls `setCamera(target)` to switch to a foreign entity. Neither is a possession handoff, and the historical click report does not prove which route ran or that ownership transferred.
- New mixins intercept the teleport packet after the server-thread handoff and foreign `setCamera` attempts. The guard reads committed lifecycle ghost, lifecycle character, and experimental ghost/character session snapshots without mutating ownership. It suppresses entity-click teleport for a committed session (even to its own body) and rejects a camera target other than the single exact owned entity; missing/ambiguous owned targets fail closed. Own-camera targeting remains allowed. No proximity shortcut or implicit Mind/body transfer. Ordinary free Spectator and actual Creative retain vanilla behavior. Suppression logs are bounded and record route, reason, camera/attempted/owned entity IDs, committed-owner count and mode.
- Reason-coded ghost termination logs distinguish trigger, diagnostic facts (including motion before/actual/resolved, displacement, bound/tolerance checks, and authority/eligibility context) and recovery result. Character terminal logs include reason/phase, epoch, camera/body identities and authority/eligibility context. A narrow read-only active-character binding check was hardened. In the earlier owner run, ghost staging failed before ghost control was established: on fresh startup `LifecycleServerContext.initialized` was final and derived from `envelope != null`; first enrollment created a primary while that flag remained false, and the earlier `LifecycleDeadClaimGhostStager.prepare` checked the stale flag. The targeted correction removes **only** that stale startup `initialized()` gate; before staging on both connected-death and reconnect paths it re-reads and validates the exact current-primary `DEAD_CLAIM`, rejecting absent, stale or corrupt evidence. A further narrow `LifecycleGhostSessionControl.serverTick` diagnostic now captures bounded per-check gate, exact-connection, level, live-ghost and Ready-timeout facts on termination; neither change alters kick/recovery policy or removes `validMotion`.
- Owner approved a **temporary operator-only Creative escape** until developer tools replace it, not a production ghost-follow, possession, role or lobby flow. Raw `/gamemode creative` during a committed lifecycle ghost is refused without kicking; `/ms14dev creative` validates and parks the exact saved `DEAD_CLAIM`, revokes and discards the transient ghost **before** switching to Creative. For a LIVING committed character, the existing `/gamemode creative` parks the exact body as durable `OFFLINE`; if that parked body later dies and the `OFFLINE` -> `DEAD_CLAIM` transition is confirmed durably saved, `/ms14dev return` now attempts a **same-connection fresh ghost** from that same corpse claim via standard staging and Begin/Ready/Commit. Death does not automatically create a ghost or switch the Creative carrier's mode; the ghost is created on return. Return pins the body, Mind, account, epoch and revision, rechecks the unique current-primary claim and lifecycle ownership, and requires the same dimension, loaded chunks and enabled gates. Raw manual Spectator is blocked for the parked carrier. Routine preflight refusal leaves Creative available for retry; an exceptional ambiguous failure may disconnect for recovery, with reconnect as fallback rather than the normal flow. No-death same-body return and ghost -> `/ms14dev creative` -> `/ms14dev return` remain supported. The lifecycle packet router retains its shared networking slot when either character or ghost handler remains. This is an implementation boundary, **not** connected gameplay verification. See [the ghost Creative escape audit](audits/2026-09-29-ghost-creative-escape.md) for unresolved safety notes.
