# Character Capabilities Sprint — Milestones and acceptance

The acceptance gates below are not complete. Targeted M0 block-source inspection and bounded M1 read-only component/actor diagnostics exist, but no action packet or world effect exists; see [the M0 block-route decision](audits/m0-block-action-route.md). A narrow mod-owned no-item block interaction first requires owner review of a body-safe target/protection/cancellation contract. Generic empty-hand mining and generic vanilla block use remain blocked on a proven body route, not on inventory. Inventory-dependent work remains gated. Development flight investigation is a separate permission-gated thread that may follow M1 if body motor authority supports it; it does not wait for M3–M5 and must not block M0–M2. Do not skip a gate based on a mock, fake player, or vanilla carrier test. Obtain owner approval before any narrowly necessary lifecycle/ownership coordination change; no lifecycle gate or vertical slice is reopened here.

## M0 — exact-version audit and action parity matrix

First inspect local cached generated NeoForge 21.1.224 / Minecraft 1.21.1 sources and platform APIs; the branch patch cited in [the provisional block-route note](audits/m0-block-action-route.md) is not verified against them. Pin callback and networking routes for every row in the action matrix below. Trace exact-session routing and both `HandActorAuthority` body paths. Record event ordering/cancellation, player assumptions, reach/ray behavior, permissions, consumption, drops and status gates. For each row, separate actually inspected SS14 source evidence from the proposed Minecraft adaptation; do not extrapolate the limited references in [architecture.md](architecture.md) into blanket parity. `InteractUsing` has caller-owned range checking. Do not claim ~1.5 SS14 units equal Minecraft blocks; owner review and platform measurement are required for any conversion. Audit the five local callback fixtures. **Pass:** reviewed, complete matrix; safe body-context route or explicit incompatibility/stop per row. No runtime edits required. **Fail/stop:** fake `ServerPlayer`, carrier authority/fallback, or unreviewed reach conversion. This note is not a completed M0 audit.

## M0G — targeted ghost-session mismatch diagnosis (early, bounded exception)

Before M1, plan reason-coded terminal diagnostics and discriminate stale/duplicate actions, correctable movement mismatch, authority loss and durable ownership disagreement. The historical post-death/reconnect movement kick has no captured reason; see [ghost-session-mismatch.md](ghost-session-mismatch.md) for evidence and policy. Keep server authority and durable ownership checks; never invent a body or permit two owners. Empirically exercise `validMotion` with real ghost locomotion/collision inputs before proposing any validator change. **Pass:** branch-specific diagnostics distinguish trigger from recovery result; controlled GameTests prove benign motion does not falsely disconnect and ambiguous authority still fails closed; owner-only connected checks are explicitly recorded as not run until performed by the owner. **Stop:** if implementation requires lifecycle ownership or previously closed lifecycle-sprint edits, obtain owner authorization and isolation first. This stage is diagnosis/policy only, not approval for a ghost rewrite or gate enablement.

## M1 — common authoritative intent admission

Build on the bounded read-only `BodyActionAuthority`/`BodyActionPolicy` groundwork: exact session, current body and epoch, live component revalidation and separate status/stun check. The optional prototype `complex_interaction` declaration seeds a persistent entity attachment once; it is not a flat per-action permission enum. Simple contact versus complex manipulation, hands/item needs and target/protection/cancellation must be determined independently; an enabled component is not permission to act. Before any world effect obtain owner review of the mod-owned body-safe target/protection/cancellation contract. Then add only justified bounded sequence/replay/rate checks and per-target revalidation hooks, not a generic hypothetical-action framework. M0/M1 are not blocked on live inventory; item-dependent work is. Missing/stale authority fails closed for ghost, absent body, non-capable body and unsupported mode. **Pass (still open):** focused JUnit and dedicated GameTests show wrong session/body/epoch, stale/replay, incapable body, stun/unusable body and invalid request reject without mutation; two competing requests cannot both commit; bounded-work/rate behavior is measured.

## M2 — block use/activation and block breaking

Implement only M0-approved routes with server-side target/face, visibility/reach, permissions/protection, status, mining progress/timing, cancellation/events and correct drops. Per [the provisional M0 block-route decision](audits/m0-block-action-route.md), a mod-owned no-item activation subset is conditional on an audited body-actor/permission adapter and owner review; it does not satisfy generic vanilla block-use acceptance. Generic empty-hand breaking is **BLOCKED** until a body route preserves mining timing/interruption, events, protection, harvest and drops; neither instant `Level.destroyBlock` nor a fake player is a substitute. Do not defer safe item-independent investigation merely for lack of live hands. Empty-hand mined-block drops may appear in the world only under a validated approved server action, but the body cannot collect them until M4 and actual item ownership are in place; there is no implicit carrier pickup. Keep held-tool mining and item-dependent block use deferred until actual hand ownership and isolation. Match explicit intent/capability policy rather than exposing all vanilla clicks. **Pass:** permitted/denied, reach/occlusion, protection, cancellation, interrupted/stale mining, drops, replay and two-client contention covered for each approved route; tool cases only after ownership gate. No client-authored instant break. Preserve the requested basic actions in final scope; owner consent is required before excluding generic routes from final acceptance.

## M3 — held item/tool use, placement, entity interaction/attack

After real hand ownership and durable Creative isolation are live, implement in-air/on-target tool use, eat/drink, placement, and only owner-approved supported entity use/attack rows. Validate active hand, actual item ownership, durability/quantity/consumption, target, permissions, status, reach and equipment/tool requirements server-side. Placement is atomic. **Pass:** every accepted row has positive, denied, canceled, stale and competing-request cases; no duplication/loss or carrier authority. Unsupported routes remain denied and explicitly enumerated.

## M4 — real ground-item pickup and drop

Blocked until live hands provide actual `ItemStack` ownership, durable preservation/isolation of Creative contents and atomic transfer. Before M4, owner must decide explicit click pickup (recommended for SS14 fidelity) versus vanilla walk-over (alternative, document parity divergence); neither is approved yet. Audit `playerTouch`, mob-loot pickup and direct/API pathways; prove narrow enforcement/suppression for the selected route. If explicit click is approved, acceptance requires no walk-over pickup for the eligible body. If walk-over is approved, document the SS14-fidelity divergence and prove server authority, no duplicate acquisition path, and collision/body ownership behavior; do not enable global auto-pickup or affect unrelated contexts. Test hand/whitelist/quantity, stale target, races, rollback and disconnect/lifecycle boundary. Server pickup proof is independent of the nonblocking final visual decision. Recommend later flat, non-bobbing visuals for SS14 mod items only, vanilla unchanged; placeholder until owner decision, no asset generation. **Pass:** one-item-one-location invariant and no Creative/unrelated regression. No grants before ownership.

## M5 — containers, crafting and action-dependent screens

Only support container/crafting interactions whose item ownership, body actor and menu/session routing are proven on this platform. Audit opening, slot transfer, close, distance loss, body/epoch change, server recipe/material checks, and vanilla inventory/container bypass. If a menu assumes a real `ServerPlayer` or opens the carrier's inventory, defer it for a narrow owner-approved UI solution; do not create an insecure substitute or imply all vanilla menus are supported. **Pass:** every accepted menu route is server-authoritative and closes/invalidates safely on session/body change; item conservation and permissions hold. Unsupported menus are enumerated.

## M6 — separately gated development grant and flight

Two independent development-only subgates, both disabled by default and excluded from basic action success; neither may delay M0–M2. Proposed grant is an audited permission-gated `/ms14dev` give-like server command targeting the exact currently owned body; it is blocked until real hand transfer and durable Creative isolation, and must never grant to the carrier inventory. Flight may be investigated after M1, without waiting for M3–M5, only if the controlled capable body has supported motor authority. Any flight is permission-gated for that body and an owner-approved motor only: no carrier Creative/gameMode bypass or carrier flight. Obtain owner approval before any movement-owner edits. Neither grants production gameplay capability. **Pass:** owner-reviewed dev-only gates, no default enablement, no admission bypass, clean disable/removal, Creative/body isolation tests. Stop for owner approval before any lifecycle/ownership coordination.

## M7 — multiplayer, lifecycle boundaries and final acceptance

Test supported actions across both lifecycle and experimental capable-CHARACTER authority paths (both are final requirements), exact-session routing, body/epoch replacement, ghost/unavailable/incomplete lifecycle, stun/death/unusable state, disconnect/reconnect and bounded conflicts. No broad lifecycle rewrite or gate enablement; seek owner approval before narrowly required coordination edits. Prove unrelated-player and Creative behavior unchanged; body inventory remains off until durable Creative preservation/isolation is separately accepted. Measure bounded work at 20 players. Connected tests are owner-only; assistant does not perform manual gameplay.

**Final full acceptance requires every row in the complete inventory below to have an owner-reviewed status and evidence.** No full acceptance claim while any row is unreviewed or unsupported without explicit owner-approved exclusion. JUnit + dedicated GameTests are necessary but do not substitute for owner-only connected acceptance of both authority paths, session routing, vanilla isolation, menus and multiplayer.
