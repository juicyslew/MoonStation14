# Connected client checks

Use a fresh disposable test world and the existing controlled-body fixture,
not the spectator carrier's inventory. Power the unit through a real APC.
Old floor-overlay disposal saves are not migrated by this replacement.

1. Press the construction key (provisional **G**) with either hand occupied or
   empty: enter Disposal Pipes without a container menu or consumed hand slot.
   Hold G and scroll to choose a pipe shape; move normally while doing so.
   Check the HUD: wrong material red, too little yellow, enough green. It must
   name Steel and the required quantity, and display actual rebound controls.
2. Hold steel and aim: check the thick ghost, dim when steel is insufficient,
   bright when ready and valid, red when blocked. **R** rotates meaningful
   orientations, preferring neighbouring connections but allowing a new run.
   Use builds in one second for two steel; repeat Use retains the shape.
   **H** exits and removes HUD/ghost. Steel alone must never open a pipe picker.
3. Build horizontal and vertical runs, including a bend. Pipes occupy their own
   blocks and face the clicked surface. Floor tiles cannot hide them. A straight
   crossing in a steel wall must leave the wall and room seal intact.
4. Put a trunk directly below the powered unit, with its route facing sideways
   or DOWN, not UP into the unit. For a DOWN inlet, aim at the unit's underside
   with a free block cell below it. Insert an item, E → engage; check the two-
   second flush, complete stack arrival, sounds and charging appearance.
5. E → Climb inside. Check half-second entry, unchanged hands/equipment and
   camera, movement escape, then self-flush through a vertical run and bend.
   No walking during transit, camera loss or stuck invisible body after exit.
6. Wrench a pipe loose, use a screwdriver to preview and confirm rotation, then
   wrench it anchored. Rotation takes one second and must not change its type.
   Try cancelling by movement/tool change. Remove a pipe with a lit welder.
   Try power loss, a broken route and a replaced wall
   host: no missing/duplicated payloads or permanently trapped passenger.
7. Unload/reload and restart normally. Check pipe shapes, contents and restored
   body flags. In a blast-safe area, an armed grenade's fuse must not pause or
   restart while held with the panel open, inserted, transported or ejected.

These connected checks remain outstanding until actually performed; automated
logic/fixture results alone do not prove client presentation or camera behavior.
