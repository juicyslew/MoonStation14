# Disposal presentation asset groundwork

Assets copied from the verified SS14 checkout at `c9df5ef5d675b0d1d226828bddf6b78c28502d91` (Space-wizards/space-station-14). Images are verbatim native 32×32 frames from `Resources/Textures/Structures/Piping/disposal.rsi`; license `CC-BY-SA-3.0`. RSI copyright metadata: CEV Eris disposal sprites modified by DrSmugleaf; signal router based on normal router modified by deltanedas; disposal bins by EmoGarbage404; X pipes by K-Dynamic; signaler based on tagger modified by Wolfkey. See upstream `meta.json` for full attribution text.

Local disposal PNGs are under `src/main/resources/assets/moonstation14/textures/block/disposal/`. Four-direction pipe states are cropped 32×32 files `_frame0` through `_frame3` in upstream RSI frame order; renderer orientation must map those indices to cardinal directions. RSI mapping: `pipe-s` straight; `pipe-b` bend; `pipe-c` corner/connection transition; `pipe-d` trunk; `pipe-j1`/`pipe-j2` junctions and `pipe-j1f`/`pipe-j2f` flipped variants; `pipe-j1s`/`pipe-j2s` plus `pipe-j1sf`/`pipe-j2sf` mirrored variants; `pipe-y`/`pipe-yf` Y and flipped Y; `pipe-x`/`pipe-xf` X and flipped X; `pipe-t`/`pipe-tf` T and flipped T; `pipe-tagger`/`pipe-tagger-partial` tagger/full and partial; `signal-router`/`signal-router-flipped` router orientations. Unit states `disposal` and `disposal-charging` are individual 32×32 frames; `condisposal` is imported for unpowered visual state. `disposal-flush_frame0..11.png` are individual frames and `disposal-flush.png` is their vertically stacked 32×384 Minecraft animation sheet. Its `.mcmeta` preserves upstream RSI delays (frames 0–5 and 7–11: 0.066 s; frame 6: 0.5 s). At 20 TPS quantization, short frames use 1 tick (0.05 s) and frame 6 uses 10 ticks (0.5 s), total 16 ticks / 0.8 s versus source 0.896 s. `disposal_unit.json` maps every `anchored`/`powered` combination and visual state: `unpowered`→`condisposal`, `ready`→`disposal`, `charging`→`disposal-charging`, and `flushed`→animated `disposal-flush`. Each uses a separate 12×16×12 box model, mapping one SS14 pixel-art frame to each face as a deliberately simple 3D box/quad adaptation, not a physical geometry reconstruction. Anchor/power combinations currently share appearance. The disposal item model remains static.

Tool icon imports follow pinned prototypes: `Entities/Objects/Tools/tools.yml` uses `wrench.rsi` state `icon` and `screwdriver.rsi` state `screwdriver`; `welders.yml` uses `welder.rsi` state `icon`. Copied native 32×32 states are `wrench.png`, `screwdriver.png`, and `welder.png`; corresponding generated item models use them directly. Their individual RSI metadata specifies CC-BY-SA-3.0. Wrench: “Taken from vgstation at commit https://github.com/vgstation-coders/vgstation13/commit/1cdfb0230cc96d0ba751fa002d04f8aa2f25ad7d, storage by EmoGarbage404 (github)”. Screwdriver: “Taken from vgstation at https://github.com/vgstation-coders/vgstation13/commit/1cdfb0230cc96d0ba751fa002d04f8aa2f25ad7d”. Welder: “Taken from tgstation at commit https://github.com/tgstation/tgstation/commit/199fffd989d6f7fd6ea9c5188c875137df4f34b8”.

Audio use is source-verified in consumers: `DisposalUnitComponent.FlushSound` defaults to `/Audio/Machines/disposalflush.ogg`; `DisposalTubeComponent.ClangSound` defaults to `/Audio/Effects/clang.ogg` with volume −5 dB. Local copied clips are under `sounds/disposal/` and have unique declarations in `sounds.json`. Their per-file terms/attributions are not resolved in pinned upstream attribution manifests; see adjacent notices and [unresolved audio register](../audio-unknown-provenance.md). No substitute audio was used.

Imported assets and renderer wiring are not connected-client acceptance: visuals, animation, rotation and audio still require the checks in `client-checks.md`.

October 4 replacement: exposed pipes now render a thick 3D mesh from resolved
prototype geometry in their own block cells, not floor quads. Native attributed
RSI frames supply mesh textures; this is a Minecraft geometry adaptation, not
an upstream 3D asset. Sealed crossing records are concealed by their unchanged
wall hosts, never by floor tiles. Construction ghosts use the same geometry.
