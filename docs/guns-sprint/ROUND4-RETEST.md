# Gun presentation retest

Restart with the technically validated candidate. Use the controlled character,
not Creative. Enable combat/harm through **Ctrl+K**, then close Actions.

1. Fire all eight guns in first person and **F5**. Watch the actual barrel tips;
   orbit a shooting character to see effects from the side and end-on. Svalinn
   should show one continuous beam, with localized muzzle/impact effects.
2. Fire moving bullets/energy bolts down a clear, loaded corridor longer than
   20 blocks. They should no longer disappear near six blocks. The two hitscan
   lasers retain their 20-block range.
3. Put a gun on the ground, aim at it and **right-click**. Use its existing controls:
   magazine/bolt, cylinder, manual cycling, cell ejection or energy-mode cycling.
   The gun stays on the ground; ejected items appear beside it. Fixed integral
   weapons have a read-only row. Picking up the gun or moving out of reach closes
   the window.
4. With a Lecter active, **Ctrl+Z** to wield, then **C** to lie down. In harm mode,
   turn while stationary and inspect in **F5**: stomach down, raised chest/head,
   two bent supporting arms, and whole-body aim facing. **K** must not leave this
   aiming stance face-up. Unwield/leave harm to check ordinary turnover again.
5. Fire a fresh Inspector six times, then pull the trigger again: six gunshot
   sounds, followed by an empty click—not a seventh bang.
6. Hold a small cell beside a gun in both hand orders (**X** switches hands).
   Charge details should match ordinary hand-detail size and look subordinate.
   Compare Svalinn's smaller size both held and on the floor.

Existing loading and charging steps remain in [MK58-MANUAL.md](MK58-MANUAL.md).
CPU previews and technical checks do not replace this native visual review or
owner acceptance.
