## Model and adaptation boundary

Each Minecraft block remains a fixed 1 m³ gas cell with six face-neighbors. At 293.15 K and 101.325 kPa, standard air is approximately 41.6 mol per cell (`pV / RT`), so 0.1% is about 0.0416 mol and 10% about 4.16 mol. Four Minecraft cells across a four-block-high room motivated the requested 800-cell processing cap and 8,000-candidate discovery cap, adapting SS14's 200 / 2,000 tile limits. Those are bounded-work limits, not proof an entire connected region was found or processed. Existing finite ownership, unloaded/unknown fail-closed behavior, and transient per-level exterior export ledger remain authoritative.

## Implemented LINDA behavior

- `LindaGasSharing` compares neighboring cell pairs against immutable cycle-start mixtures, but applies qualifying transfers sequentially to current live inventories. Candidate active cells use deterministic position order and neighbors use fixed down/up/north/south/west/east order; each pair is visited at most once per pass. The owner's explicit choice was sequential live transfer in fixed topology order, rather than snapshot-to-snapshot commit. This is reproducible for the same ordering but order-dependent.
- For each gas, eligibility uses strict `abs(firstArchivedMoles - secondArchivedMoles) > standard 1 m³ cell moles * 0.001` and strict relative difference `> firstArchivedMoles * 0.001`. That sample-denominator and zero handling follows the upstream comparison convention; direction/order can make this relative check asymmetric. Temperature-only comparison requires archived temperature difference `> 4 K` and sufficient gas inventory. Previously grouped pairs bypass the normal comparison gate.
- A qualifying pair shares each species' live mole difference divided by `connectedNeighbors(current) + 1`; heat uses the 0.4 open-transfer coefficient and live thermal state. Species and energy remain conserved in the closed finite LINDA kernel. This is intentionally order-dependent. The threshold equality and mixed-gas/energy behavior have focused pure coverage.
- Changed Monstermos cells are deferred from LINDA until the next eligible service tick, preventing same-due-tick double-processing. When a patch changes cells on a due group-cycle tick, group advancement is postponed to the next available 12-tick window so those same-due-tick cells are not also group-processed; this intentional scheduling safeguard is a parity deviation. The service also respects existing per-tick active-cell, edge, inspection, and scheduler work caps.

## Implemented Monstermos-inspired finite routing

- Normal routing discovers a connected finite patch, capped at 800 applied cells and 8,000 discovery candidates, and uses deterministic directed graph-connected donor-to-taker routes toward patch-average total moles. Unlike upstream's attempt to move the full donor surplus, each invocation is limited to 15% of a donor's initial surplus; long routes retain half a packet in an intermediate cell. These deliberate caps create gradual, spatially distributed plumes. Species mixtures and enthalpy travel together, and closed-route mass/energy are conserved. This is not SS14's algorithm: upstream's normal solver uses tile-level equalization, whereas this kernel routes packets along graph paths. Equal-pressure pure O2/N2 rooms therefore require LINDA for species mixing; normal Monstermos does not create an arbitrary total-pressure gradient.
- If the patch has verified exterior faces, a separate space kernel starts at exterior-adjacent finite cells and routes inventory inward by graph distance. The 0.15 escape ratio and pressure/wind-cap approximations remain, but the local kernel has no 2 mol minimum-inventory snap; tiny positive tails may drain over repeated work until the 1e-6 convergence epsilon is reached. A 10 kPa release-pressure threshold coexists with low-pressure spacing decay. These rules approximate the upstream `ReleaseGasTo`/pressure behavior amid inconsistent source units and the Minecraft boundary model; do not claim exact upstream pressure semantics. Species and thermal energy exported through this boundary are recorded once in the existing transient service ledger. No pressure wind/impulse is applied to entities.
- Service discovery and writes remain bounded, use existing priority/FIFO/retry lanes, avoid force-loading, and fail closed across unknown/unloaded boundaries. A changed Monstermos patch is protected from LINDA processing during that same due service cycle.

## Implemented excited-group behavior

- Groups form on LINDA-qualified sharing (including temperature-only qualifying comparisons), merge when connected through a qualifying share, and are transient server-level state. Excited groups are enabled by default within the enabled atmosphere service. Tracking and individual groups are capped at 800 cells; over-cap formation/add/merge is declined rather than growing without bound. Group membership is not persisted and is rebuilt through later activity after a reset/restart; gas and finite ownership persistence are separate concerns.
- A `LastShare` moved-moles amount strictly greater than 10% of standard cell moles (~4.16 mol) resets both cooldowns; strictly greater than 0.0416 mol resets only dismantle cooldown. These are distinct triggers.
- Excited groups are not merely additional per-tick LINDA time: they are transient LINDA-qualified pairs/groups that bypass the regular pair comparison gate and are advanced in their own group cycle. The atmosphere service runs every two Minecraft game ticks (10 Hz at 20 TPS); per-invocation work budgets were reduced from the prior four-tick cadence to retain approximate per-second work counts. The service advances groups once per 12 Minecraft game ticks—six service due cycles, or 0.6 seconds at 20 TPS. SS14 configured at 15 TPS processes staged work; that does not mean a full LINDA-plus-Monstermos pass per stage or establish parity with this local cycle. Group cycles remain a local `perFullCycle` approximation.
- Breakdown is requested after stage >4 (fifth counted full group pass), but averaging commits only when every cell is within 5% of standard 41.6 mol per species (about 2.08 mol) of its computed group average and within 4 K of average temperature. Otherwise the group defers breakdown instead of instantly erasing visible gradients. Dismantling happens after stage >16 (seventeenth counted pass), deactivating membership without group averaging. Space is not all-consuming during averaging. Stale/unloaded tracked membership invalidates transient group state; bounded/atomic pending breakdown avoids partial averaging.
