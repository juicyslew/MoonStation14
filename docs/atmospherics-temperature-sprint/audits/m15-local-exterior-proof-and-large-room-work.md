## Equalization discussion context

- Current normal Monstermos computes the selected bounded connected patch's average total moles and routes the full donor surplus toward takers along face-connected paths. There is no intermediate per-hop falloff; an intermediate cell may nevertheless end with 0 mol. Packets carry their species mixture and enthalpy. This path-routed transfer is not a global mixture clone or a universal percentage applied to every edge. LINDA separately performs local species mixing. Space flow's 0.6 mol inbound-retention behavior is a separate rule and should not be conflated with normal equalization.
- SS14's normal equalization also targets average moles using face-connected routes, with normal 200 / hard 2,000 limits. Similar high-level intent does not make the current kernel exact SS14 parity. This implementation uses Minecraft 1 m³ cells and binary face connectivity, an 800-cell patch / 8,000-candidate cap (not SS14's limits), one job per server level, and 384 ownership probes per due step. The near-uniform excited-group guard means group breakdown/averaging is not unconditional as in SS14. Space flow uses 0.15 of own inventory plus inbound demand, retains at most 0.6 mol of inbound gas in an underfilled interior cell, and caps exports by local boundary-cell pressure; these differ from SS14 wind. Runtime behavior and test acceptance remain unresolved. Do not characterize either implementation as globally cloning a mixture or applying one universal transfer percentage.
- **Decision — keep the current normal Monstermos; do not implement cached average-and-nudge now.** The proposed alternative is a cached connected-component average-and-nudge of total moles, with conservative actual donor-species and thermal-energy redistribution and, optionally, a very limited local source-texture term. It could make gas processing faster, approximately O(N gases), but a naive shared pool erases event-source and multi-source local gradients and species provenance. Cache invalidation would need to occur on true connectivity changes only: passability/topology, chunk availability/lifecycle, or exterior ownership changes. More importantly, paired amount accounting without explicit paths changes local packet routing and does not naturally express different capacities at narrow versus wide openings. Any future design must treat opening-specific boundary flux and pressure/space-wind as separate work, preserve every gas species and thermal energy conservatively, and verify an actual space case. Do not alter the currently working vent plume. For now, current path-connected Monstermos remains the best available SS14-inspired option; no code or behavior change is authorized or claimed here.
- These are algorithm design trade-offs, not a diagnosis or fix for the deferred finite-zero observation. The cache and average-and-nudge remain future-only discussion, not an implemented optimization.

## Large-room diagnosis and next work

- **Correction: the 800-cell patch cut does not explain this reported approximately 124-cell room**, assuming those finite cells are connected as measured. A >800-cell connected region can still be split into disjoint BFS patches and treated like separate sealed rooms; that is a separate general limitation, not a diagnosis of this observation.
- Ownership search has a 384-probe-per-due-step budget and at most 128 claims per claim pass. LINDA is bounded at 64 pairs with 10 active sources due per pass. These limits can constrain progress but are not, by themselves, a confirmed cause here.
- **The pure solver work-cap suspicion is not demonstrated by the available fixtures.** A deterministic 31x2x2 (124-cell) hallway fixture with 2 mol of TRITIUM at one end equalized all 20 distant cells on its first equalization, delivering 0.016129... mol to each. Work completed (`work.complete=true`) at edge-search work 7,749/8,000 and transfers 1,984/8,000, with TRITIUM and energy conserved. A separate pure fixture modeled as the 4x5x3 + 4x4x4 room also completed on first/next worker passes at 7,749 work. Thus the 8,000 solver cap was not hit in these fixtures; this does not prove runtime service behavior or exclude other topologies.
- A new isolated service GameTest, `AtmosphereRoomGameTests.longHallwayServiceDiffusesTritiumAcrossAll124Cells`, preclassifies all 124 cells finite before injecting gas, then asserts every far cell becomes positive within 10 due passes, conservation, and no ownership ledger. At the time this audit was written it had compiled but not run, using an assumed small empty template. The later isolated run used a valid 36x5x8 empty fixture and passed; see M16. This does not establish the deferred finite-zero owner observation is fixed.
- The overlay threshold was a possible explanation for a *visual* front, but it cannot explain the owner's tool reading exactly 0 mol in far cells. Do not state overlay visibility as the explanation for this authoritative zero. O2/N2 invisibility is likewise not a cause for this TRITIUM reading.
- The cause remains undiagnosed and the finite-zero hallway observation is deferred at the user's request. It remains intermittently reported; do not claim it is fixed, start or imply a runtime investigation is in progress, infer a cause from the pure fixture or later GameTest pass, or turn a proposed diagnosis into a confirmed fix.
- SS14's normal soft/hard limits are 200/2,000, while its active tiles are per-grid and invalidated coordinates do not map directly to this implementation's single ownership job and global ownership model. It also operates on actual adjacency/face routes toward average moles; do not infer global mixture cloning or a universal percentage-transfer rule. These values and mechanics are comparison context, not a parity claim or a drop-in configuration target.
- Deferred, hypothetical proposal (not implemented): an explicit bounded seam-crossing active-tile incremental solver for large or incomplete patches. Add focused coverage including an 801+ cell room before making claims about this separate >800-cell behavior. It is not currently a proposed explanation or fix for the measured 124-cell room.
