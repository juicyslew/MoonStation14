## Delivered simulation facts and limits

- Service due cadence is every 2 game ticks (10 Hz at 20 TPS), not 10 guaranteed solves per region per second. Ownership discovery is capped at 384 probes/due step; a Monstermos patch at 800 cells and candidate discovery at 8,000. One Monstermos job per server level at a time; priority/FIFO scheduling, queue/memory bounds, ownership, LINDA, retries, and scheduler budgets constrain progress. Above the 800-cell patch limit, disjoint BFS patches are currently treated like separate sealed rooms; the planned bounded seam-crossing incremental solver is not implemented.
- Producer/sink amount: up to 2 mol/step (20 mol/s nominal). Heater: 40,000 J/step (400,000 J/s nominal). These are configured rates, not measured room/outlet rates or performance evidence.
- Five client gas overlays: plasma, tritium, water vapor, ammonia, frezon. Current SS14-derived concentration policy is scaled from a 2.5 m³ reference into a 1 m³ cell; default local 2 mol reaches full alpha (ammonia/frezon have their own thresholds/maxima). Alpha uses 20 levels; presentation is unverified and not harmful/ignition logic.
- SS14 reaction eligibility reference: local tritium and oxygen each at least 0.01 mol, temperature >=373.149 K. Local implementation has no fire/reaction; gas composition/respiration remain inactive. Do not claim reaction, physiology, or SS14 parity.
- LINDA comparison is archived-comparison/sequential-current (in-place) species exchange: it locally mixes gas species. Equal-total-moles O2/N2 does not mix via Monstermos; LINDA handles species mixing.
- Retain current normal Monstermos as the best currently available SS14-inspired behavior. It computes a bounded connected patch's average total moles and routes the full donor surplus toward that average along face-connected paths; there is no 0.15 donor-surplus cap or artificial 50% intermediate retention. Species and enthalpy travel with each packet. This is not a global mixture clone or a universal percentage-transfer rule; an intermediate cell may end a pass at 0 mol. The excited-group near-uniform gas/temperature gate remains deliberate; unlike SS14, group averaging is not unconditional on the fifth cycle.
- This is parity-inspired, not exact SS14 parity: Minecraft uses 1 m³ binary face geometry, an 800-cell patch / 8,000-candidate bound (versus SS14 normal 200 / hard 2,000), and one job per level with 384 ownership probes per due step. The near-uniform excited-group guard deviates from unconditional excited-group breakdown. Space flow uses 0.15 of own inventory plus inbound demand, 0.6 mol interior inbound retention, and a local boundary pressure cap; these differ from SS14 wind. Owner connected-client acceptance and full SS14 parity remain unverified.
- The isolated server GameTest assertions passed 154/154 required tests for that batch; this does not resolve owner acceptance or current connected-client/visual smoke, which remain unverified. See the [M16 evidence audit](audits/m16-server-gametest-and-acceptance-evidence.md) for scope.
- A cached connected-component “average-and-nudge” of total moles was considered and explicitly not selected for now. Keep the current normal Monstermos kernel; a cached shared-pool approach may reduce work toward O(N gases), but risks losing event-source/multi-source local gradients and species provenance. Any future version would need topology caching invalidated only by genuine connectivity changes (passability/topology, chunk lifecycle/availability, or ownership), preserve gas species and thermal energy through verified conservative donor redistribution, and use separate opening-specific boundary flux/pressure handling for narrow and wide doorways and space-wind behavior. A very limited local source-texture term was optional in the proposal, not a current feature. Do not change the currently working vent plume or claim the proposal is implemented. A verified actual space case is required before asserting its behavior.
- Monstermos space flow uses 0.15 × each tile's own initial inventory plus inbound child demand, routed toward verified openings; the 0.15 is not reapplied to inbound packets at every hop. Underfilled interior cells retain at most 0.6 mol of inbound gas each, which can delay flow through a newly empty corridor as cells fill. This cap is specific to space-flow routing; normal room equalization still routes the selected patch's full donor surplus. Export uses the current boundary-cell mixture/pressure, with a local pressure-derived cap, not global room-average pressure. Immutable-boundary fallback is distinct and uses only current boundary-cell pressure. Both avoid the two-mol cleanup floor and retain the last residual to a 1e-6 mol epsilon without hard-zero/refill blink.
