# Atmospherics and Temperature Sprint

## Purpose, scope, and current status

This document tracks the experimental Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge 21.1.224 world-gas foundation and its acceptance gates. Sparse server-cell storage, a default-off startup gate, devices, a handheld analyzer, local exchange, direct-sky exterior handling, and a bounded equalization path are present in code. M6–M11 prototype code is also present as described below, but code presence does not mean milestone acceptance. The M12 audit documents current cadence, plume, opacity, and thermal/reaction limitations. After the latest immutable-boundary fallback change, the coordinator reported `.\gradlew.bat test --rerun-tasks --no-daemon` and `.\gradlew.bat build --no-daemon` both `BUILD SUCCESSFUL`; `compileGametestJava` executed during the full test rerun, and the build was successful/up-to-date. This is compile/test/build evidence only, not runtime or owner acceptance; GameTests were compiled, not executed. **M0–M12 acceptance remains OPEN.** M11 pure kernels, service integration, and deterministic scenario tests exist, but code presence does not establish acceptance; server-world GameTests have not been executed. No SS14 parity, comprehensive transactionality, owner smoke, visual runtime verification, or 20-player performance result is claimed. Respiration is a no-op; entity gas composition is not yet physiologically active. `Oxygenate` remains unsupported; `AdjustTemperature` has a thermal handler for supported characters.

`enableAtmospherics` is a COMMON config setting and defaults to `false`. It and `atmosphereVacuumDimensions` are sampled at `ServerAboutToStartEvent`; changing either requires restart. While disabled, the atmosphere service does no reads, writes, or processing. Device tickers still make a cheap enabled check. The atmosphere service cadence is every two Minecraft game ticks (10 Hz at 20 TPS), not the prior four-tick cadence. Budgets were halved per invocation relative to that prior cadence to preserve approximate per-second throughput; this is not a measured performance result, and an 800-cell patch write may still concentrate significant work in one tick. The configured server rate and real load are unmeasured. `atmosphereVacuumDimensions` optionally lists dimensions whose immutable directly sky-exposed ambient is vacuum; an empty/default list makes exposed ambient breathable in every dimension, including the End and custom dimensions. Covered cells remain finite in either kind of dimension. Prefer an isolated new world/dimension for testing. Changing policy in a saved world can reinterpret cells and needs an owner-approved migration plan.

Example `config/moonstation14-common.toml` settings (restart after changes):

```toml
enableAtmospherics = true
atmosphereVacuumDimensions = ["minecraft:the_end"]
```

## Current model and boundaries

- A Minecraft block position is one fixed 1 m³ gas cell. Pressure is derived from moles and temperature using the ideal-gas relation; mixtures have gas-dependent heat capacity and thermal energy.
- Cells without explicit chunk-attachment overrides read dimension ambient: 21% oxygen / 79% nitrogen at 293.15 K and 101.325 kPa by default, or zero-gas nominal 2.7 K ambient in configured vacuum dimensions. Ownership is separate from stored gas: directly exposed sky seeds are exterior unless a persistent finite claim takes precedence; covered unclaimed cells are initially UNKNOWN while a resumable search determines sky connectivity or enclosure.
- There is **no permanent station envelope, room object, identity, or aggregate**. New rooms/buildings may be constructed anywhere. A room is not automatically filled as a unit: cells inherit ambient until changed, and changed cells are independent overrides. Gas and energy exchange only across open six-neighbor faces; diagonal corner contact does not connect cells. Pressure is derived, not independently diffused.
- The `MOTION_BLOCKING` heightmap is only a direct-exterior seed heuristic for loaded, passable cells at/above first-free Y; it does not inspect skylight. A roof alone does not establish finite ownership. A covered unclaimed cell starts a resumable six-face `AtmosphereOwnershipSearch`: finding an open sky seed classifies visited cells as exterior, while only a complete loaded, fully enclosed proof yields finite claims. Unloaded/unknown boundaries and candidate saturation fail closed (UNKNOWN/SATURATED), not finite. Claims take precedence over the heightmap and persist in chunk data; an open claimed cell therefore remains finite after a door breach. Direct sky claim remains finite to prevent immediate gas deletion, but adjacent open sky is an exchange sink. There is no permanent envelope or room column; sky-connected air beneath an overhang can be exterior. This is not exact SS14 vacuum behavior: heightmap heuristics may misclassify custom geometry, topology edits may displace/reinterpret matter, and there is no exact SS14 floor/map identity.
- Exchange into direct exterior exports species and thermal energy from finite cells to a transient per-server-level ledger. This ledger is not persisted; its long-term accounting/persistence policy remains open. Unloaded chunks are unavailable/closed, not exterior sinks.
- Ownership work stages claims at up to 128 cells and probes up to 384 cells per due tick, with a 131,072-candidate ownership cap. Gas processing is separate: bounded Monstermos-inspired patches use up to 800 finite cells and an 8,000-candidate discovery limit, alongside local neighbor species/heat processing. The relevant per-invocation work budgets were halved when cadence moved from four to two game ticks to preserve approximate per-second counts. These are bounded approximations, not an SS14 port or parity claim; they do not bound all tick cost or establish the 20-player target.
- Priority mutation seeds with FIFO interleaving, an 8,192-position hot queue, coalesced per-chunk dirty retries, and a version-checked ordered `AtmosphereChunkData.nextAfter` cursor bound some work. Bounded UNKNOWN/SATURATED ownership searches release their active job so other room seeds can run; the affected region remains unknown and is retried when chunk availability or topology changes. The ownership candidate cap can still leave a large region unresolved. The dirty cursor and per-column index changes have focused unit coverage; priority overflow/recovery and long-region fairness have unit coverage only. These do not establish bounded total scheduler memory or real-world fairness under all overload conditions. Retry work is capped per tick; dirty cursor restarts after version changes.
- Gas-only heat transport is part of the atmosphere simulation. A limited M9 body-temperature exposure prototype is also present (see below); it does not activate gas-composition physiology. Solids, pressure forces, fire/reactions, and pipes remain unmodeled. No concurrency rollback or comprehensive transactional all-or-nothing guarantee is claimed. No 20-player benchmark has been run.

## SS14 comparison (reference, not a parity target)

SS14 has map/grid atmosphere ownership and distinct LINDA and Monstermos strategies. This project has sparse fixed-volume Minecraft block cells, heightmap exterior seeds plus connectivity-proven persistent finite claims, a local pairwise path, and a bounded total-moles patch equalizer inspired by Monstermos. The different topology, ambient ownership, partial work semantics, and lifecycle mean matching a high-level solver idea does not establish equivalent behavior. Do not say vacuum behaves exactly like SS14 or claim full SS14 parity. The [M2 audit](audits/m2-2026-09-26-sky-boundary-and-equalizer.md) is an archived earlier baseline; see the [M3 ownership audit](audits/m3-dynamic-space-ownership.md) for current details and gates.

## Implemented foundation (code presence is not acceptance)

- Core validated `GasMixture`, stable `GasType` IDs, ideal-gas pressure, pair-transfer math, and bounded gas equalization under `src/main/java/com/juicyslew/moonstation14/ms14/atmos/core/`.
- Nine hard-coded gas IDs corresponding to SS14 gas names; capacities are local, not dynamically loaded from SS14 YAML.
- Sparse `ATMOSPHERE_CHUNK` attachment, schema-v2 codec with persisted finite claims (legacy schema-v1 gas overrides promote to finite claims), `sample`, positive `addGas`, `addEnergy`, and producer/sink APIs including generic proportional `removeGasUpTo`; no species-selective extraction API.
- Server startup configuration and atmosphere event hooks; simulation is gated by `enableAtmospherics`, default false. This code path is not evidence of server integration acceptance.
- Four registered placeholder devices (breathable-air producer, proportional gas sink, heater, cooler) plus the handheld analyzer: five atmosphere items appear across the custom creative tabs. Placeholder device/analyzer assets use vanilla textures.
- Empty-hand right-click on devices requests a server-chat front-cell diagnostic readout; the handheld analyzer samples server-side and reports through the shared formatter. Loaded passable UNKNOWN cells may show numeric **PROVISIONAL/FALLBACK** values (stored gas if present, otherwise dimension ambient); these are diagnostic presentation only, not authoritative physical state. Do not infer ownership or change devices/world state from them: strict samples, writes, and simulation remain fail-closed, and device changes are blocked until ownership is proven. A solid closed door has no gas in its block cell: analyzer use on it targets the clicked-face adjacent passable cell, readable when that adjacent cell is available. If it is not a loaded gas cell, the response is explicitly “No gas cell on the clicked side,” not an invented door atmosphere. World behavior and multiplayer interactions remain unverified.
- Fixed-rate device behavior remains gated and runs every two game ticks: producer adds up to 2 mol of breathable mix per step (20 mol/s at 20 TPS), taking a partial dose when necessary to stay at or below 202.65 kPa; sink removes up to 2 mol proportionally (20 mol/s); heater adds 40,000 J per step (400,000 J/s at 20 TPS); cooler removes at most 40,000 J per step and floors at 2.7 K. These are source/rule rates, not measured-world performance or integration results.
- Chunk load events defer coordinates until `FULL`; post-tick makes at most 16 non-forced lookups to resume saved overrides. Lifecycle ordering remains unverified by executed integration GameTests.
- Runtime includes ownership discovery/claims (384 probes and 128 claim writes per due tick; 131,072 ownership candidates), local pairwise exchange, and separately bounded gas patches (800 cells / 8,000 discovery candidates). Roof/door service GameTests compiled but were NOT EXECUTED; no manual launch or 20-player benchmark occurred. See M3 audit for limits and partial-claim restart risk.

Matter behavior on topology edits is unresolved; no exact vacuum or room classification claim.
4. **M3 — Devices, analyzer, and producer/sink API (CODE IMPLEMENTED; ACCEPTANCE OPEN).** Four test devices, APIs, and handheld analyzer code exist. Code registration confirms five atmosphere items in the custom Moonstation creative tabs. Verify device/analyzer interactions, real server-world effects, and owner smoke. Pure tests do not establish integration behavior.
5. **M4 — Scale and performance (OPEN).** Measure scheduler and 800-cell preflight/write cost, bounded queue/retry memory and latency; test progress through connected regions larger than the 8,000-candidate discovery limit; benchmark at or above 20 players. No profile or benchmark exists.
6. **M5 — Reference parity matrix and remaining-system gates (OPEN).** Build behavior-by-behavior comparison with deviations and evidence. Fire, pipes, respiration/composition effects, chemistry, and other parity claims require separate implementation and tests. The partial M9 thermal prototype does not establish full body-system parity. No full SS14 parity claim is justified.
7. **M6 — Moon sky presentation (CODE PRESENT; ACCEPTANCE OPEN).** A client-only, fixed Overworld-effects renderer draws procedural black sky/stars, a white sun and a blue Earth placeholder. `moonSky` is a client-local config defaulting false, independent of server `enableAtmospherics` and separate from server atmospherics; it does not change time, daylight, block light, skylight, or atmosphere. For a visual smoke check the user must enable `moonSky` in the client-side config; changing only the server atmospherics setting will not enable the sky. The mod-bus registration exists, but no in-game render verification has occurred. See the M6–M9 implementation audit.
8. **M7 — Per-gas generators and complete analyzer list (CODE PRESENT; ACCEPTANCE OPEN).** Nine single-gas producers coexist with the mixed-air producer and are listed in creative tabs. They share the existing block entity and use a pressure-capped 2 mol per two-game-tick step (20 mol/s at 20 TPS; partial when needed to remain at or below 202.65 kPa); the analyzer formatter enumerates all present gases at significant-digit precision. All nine producers and the direct tritium visuals currently have no chemical/physiological harm effects; gas composition remains inactive. See the audit; game-world interactions remain unverified.
9. **M8 — Client-only gas visuals (CODE PRESENT; ACCEPTANCE OPEN).** Five gases only (plasma, tritium, water vapor, ammonia, frezon) are visualized; nitrous oxide has no overlay. Visibility thresholds preserve SS14 concentration scaled from 2.5 m³ to 1 m³, but opacity intentionally saturates at the requested 40 mol per Minecraft block rather than SS14's 5/7/12 mol maxima; alpha is quantized to 20 levels. Three crossed planes are opacity-limited through a four-block column. Chunk-watch snapshots and targeted deltas are rate-limited to 3 Hz, with client cache staging and resync when bounded change tracking overflows. The client draws procedural translucent quads, up to 2,000 cells within 48 blocks, selecting nearest candidates first and favoring tritium only at equal distance. A HUD warning indicates when bounded cache eviction makes the view incomplete. These visuals are presentation only: direct tritium visuals currently cause no chemical harm and have no ignition-safety meaning. No SS14 sprites were copied; current UI-color tint approximation is not the upstream white-alpha sprite/animation. Runtime appearance is unverified. See attribution and limitations in the audit.
10. **M9 — Entity temperature and gas effects (PARTIAL CODE PRESENT; ACCEPTANCE OPEN).** A typed character-state attachment/provider bridge, one-second temperature exposure and `DamageSystem` heat/cold damage exist for supported human characters (310.15 K baseline; 42 J/kgK and approximately 70 kg; thresholds 325/260 K). `AdjustTemperature` has a real handler. Current thermal coupling uses atmosphere temperature/heat capacity, but heat/cold threshold damage also applies from stored body state independently of a gas sample. The spectator controller is inert. Respiration remains a no-op; gas composition does not cause physiology, and toxic effects, tritium/fire, pressure/wind, organs, and oxygen saturation are deferred. Do not claim gases fully affect entities.
11. **M10 — Breach outflow and overlay regressions (CODE PRESENT; ACCEPTANCE OPEN).** Finite-to-vacuum immutable-boundary fallback (used when a Monstermos job is unavailable) uses half-pressure-difference outflow above 10 kPa; at or below 10 kPa it drains half the gas per step and snaps only at/below 1e-6 mol. It does not use SS14's immediate two-mol minimum-inventory cleanup. Monstermos space flow instead uses a 0.15 fraction and the same 1e-6 mol epsilon. Neither path has visible hard-zero/refill-to-zero behavior; gas and heat ledger deltas exactly reflect finite-cell before/after changes. An urgent opening-adjacent work lane is capped at 256 positions with at most eight consecutive urgent polls before regular FIFO work. The `AtmosphereRoomGameTests` source compares a full-wall opening with a single-block opening, but these GameTests have NOT been executed in a server world. Client overlay changes reduce compounded opacity across three planes/four-block columns and keep quads inset; visual synchronization separates non-reset deltas from reset snapshots and replays newer deltas after staged snapshots. The coordinator-reported full test rerun and build after the latest immutable-boundary fallback change passed (see evidence below). No in-game appearance/flicker check or 20-player performance result exists. This is a Minecraft approximation, not directional Monstermos wind or exact SS14 behavior. See the [M10 regression audit](audits/m10-breach-and-overlay-regressions.md).
12. **M11 — LINDA exchange and Monstermos / excited-group behavior (CODE IMPLEMENTED; ACCEPTANCE OPEN).** Server-only pure kernels and `AtmosphereService` integration implement archived-comparison/sequential-live LINDA, bounded normal Monstermos routing, inward space routing, and transient excited groups enabled by default when atmosphere simulation is enabled. The current service cadence is every two game ticks (10 Hz at 20 TPS), with at most 10 active LINDA sources, six face checks per source, and a 64-face-check due-step cap. Normal Monstermos sends at most 15% of donor surplus per due step and retains packet gas in intermediate cells on long routes; excited-group final averaging is conditional on near-uniform gas and temperature, not unconditional. Monstermos vacuum tails drain by 0.15 per step toward a 1e-6 mol epsilon; when its job is unavailable, immutable-boundary fallback uses half-pressure-difference outflow above 10 kPa and half-gas drainage at/below 10 kPa, snapping only at/below that same epsilon. Neither route applies SS14's immediate two-mol cleanup. These are deliberate game-feel departures, not an SS14 port or parity claim; see the M12 audit for device, opacity and thermal/reaction limits. The coordinator-reported full test rerun and build after the latest fallback change passed (see evidence below). `AtmosphereM11GameTests` compiled but were NOT EXECUTED in a server world. See the [M11 architecture/implementation note](architecture/m11-linda-monstermos-excited-groups.md), archived [M11 baseline audit](audits/m11-implemented-kernels-and-integration.md), and [M12 cadence/plumes/opacity/thermal audit](audits/m12-cadence-plumes-opacity-and-thermal.md).
