Wall-mounted gas_vent, gas_scrubber, and air_alarm models face north in their base geometry. Minecraft's `BlockModelRotation` applies the inverse X rotation: `x=270` places the plate on UP and `x=90` on DOWN. This adapts the north-facing model to Minecraft's vertical facing variants; horizontal Y rotations and model UVs remain unchanged. The orientation test checks the actual resources and the rotated thin-plate bounds against the runtime mounting planes.
